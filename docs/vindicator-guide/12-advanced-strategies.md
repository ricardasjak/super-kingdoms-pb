# Chapter 12: Advanced Strategies

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/12_advstrategies.html`

While beginner players follow standard balanced builds, experienced veterans often utilize specialized or asymmetric playstyles to gain decisive advantages.

## 1. The Residenceless Strategy
In this build, the player dismantles or avoids building all residences, relying entirely on **Star Mines** for revenue and barracks for military housing.

- **Immunity to Arson:** Star Mines cannot be targeted or destroyed by arson operations.
- **Immunity to Kidnapping:** Having 0 civilians neutralizes enemy kidnapping ops.
- **Zero Civilian Power Drain:** Eliminates the 0.35 power/hour civilian consumption, drastically freeing up powerplants.
- **Drafting Limitation:** Because you have no civilians, you cannot draft new soldiers daily. Players stockpiled enormous soldier pools before transitioning to residenceless, or sustained their military purely from captured land and mine revenue.

## 2. The Probe Farm (Espionage Specialist)
Dedicating 20–30% of total land to Probe Factories:
- Produces tens of thousands of probes weekly.
- Operates as a sector intelligence hub (supplying continuous SoM reports on all targets).
- Executes coordinated economic warfare: burning down enemy powerplants (triggering -20% blackouts) right before sector allies landgrab.

## 3. The Pure Dragoon Offensive Spammer
Focusing military entirely on Dragoons (offense 5, cost 450) and Troopers (offense 4, cost 350):
- Highest offensive power generated per money spent.
- Sacrifices home defense entirely, requiring fast hit-and-run strikes with multiple War Leaders and relying on sector teammates to cover defense.

## 4. The Oceanic Fortress (Turtler)
- Built on Oceanic (+20% passive defense).
- Military stacked with Laser Troopers (4 defense), Laser Dragoons (5 defense), and Tanks (9 defense).
- Rarely attacks, functioning as an uncrackable bank and defense anchor for an alliance.
