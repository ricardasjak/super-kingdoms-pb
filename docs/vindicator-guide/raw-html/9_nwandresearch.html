Net Worth shows how well you have used your land and resources to build up your kingdom and a good army. Research are different bonuses (usually based on your amount of land) which you use scientists to increase.

Net Worth (NW)
When looking for possible targets, most people compare a kingdom's net worth with their amount of land. Someone with 3,000 land and 500,000 Net Worth is most likely a far better target than someone with 3,000 land and 900,000 Net Worth, since most of the net worth is made up by a kingdom's army. A recommended nw/land ratio is about 100 during the first days, 150 after a week, 200 after two weeks, and at least 250 after a month of playing. However, there is nothing wrong with having a lower ratio if you are just able to defend your land.

Research
It is good to have at least as many scientists as you have land most of the time after you’ve got more than 1,000 land, since most of the researches are based on your amount of land, and it gets harder to keep them up, the more land you gain. Here is the formula used to calculate how many research points that you need to get max. in all the land-based researches:
(If you have 1,000 land, you will need 1,000x1,000x0.01=10,000 research points for full population bonus, for example.)
ResearchPtsForMax = Land * Land * ResearchFieldFactor
ResearchFieldFactor =
Population Bonus (max 20%): 0.01 - increases amount of civilians which can live in each residence.
Power Bonus (max 50%): 0.006 - increases power production by powerplants.
Reactor Warp Core Efficiency (max 20%, needs Energy Core to become available in the research room): 0.01 - reduces the attack time by up to 20%.
Military Bonus (max 30%): 0.0063 - bonus to offense and defense
Money Bonus (max 25%): 0.011 - bonus to income from mines and taxes
Frequency Decryption Center (max 25%): 0.017 - gives you FDC% probability to make it possible for you to see who probed your kingdom, also for successful probings.

Some planet types also get bonuses or penalties to their research needed. If your kingdom is built on an Oceanic planet, multiply the ResearchPtsForMax with 1,1 to get the real research points needed for max.

There are also several researches which are not land based. Those let you train or build other, more powerful things and units, like Tactical Fighters, when you have a specific amount of research points researched in them:
Dragoons (60,000 points) - makes it possible for you to train Dragoons, powerful offensive military units.
Laser Dragoons (72,000 points) - makes it possible for you to train Laser Dragoons, powerful defensive military units.
Fusion Technology (30,000 points) - makes it possible for you to construct Fusion powerplants on your land. Also makes it possible to construct Fusion missiles and start researching energy core.
S.C.O.U.T.E.R (15,000 points) - gives you the attack time for all kingdoms, in the list in the attack room, and on other locations (for example the sectorlist).
Vesudian Core and AI Technology (600,000 points) - makes it possible to build Air support bays and construct AI controlled Tactical Fighters.
Energy Core (50,000 points + Fusion Technology finished) - makes it possible to construct energy missiles, increases power production by nuclear and fusion powerplants by 20%, makes Reactor Warp Core Efficiency Research available, and decreases attack time by 10%.
Probe Armour (92,000 points) - all probe losses are reduced by 50%.
