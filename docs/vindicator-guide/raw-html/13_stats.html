Strategy: FTB had 1.5 million soldiers saved up when he went residenceless. He had over 900k probes earlier, but was down to 581k at the end. What makes his kingdom unique was first of all that it was almost only star mines, since he was both residenceless and powerless. Secondly, he never researched Tactical Fighters. This is the only time I am aware about that a residenceless top player at the end of a game hasn't had that.
