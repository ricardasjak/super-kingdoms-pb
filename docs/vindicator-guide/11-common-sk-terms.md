# Chapter 11: Common SK Terms

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/11_skterms.html`

A glossary of terms and abbreviations used throughout the Star Kingdoms community:

- **AIM:** AOL Instant Messenger, the primary external real-time chat client used by Star Kingdoms alliances and sectors.
- **Arson:** Probe sabotage mission that burns and destroys enemy buildings.
- **Bank:** A kingdom that stockpiles enormous amounts of liquid cash (often with minimal defense), making them an attractive target for robs or landgrabs.
- **BCart / BCartfall:** The original creator and administrator of Star Kingdoms.
- **Drags / LDs:** Dragoons (offensive cavalry unit) / Laser Dragoons (defensive infantry unit).
- **Farm:** A weakened, inactive, or undefended kingdom that is repeatedly attacked for easy land or resources.
- **FDC:** Frequency Decryption Center, the research project that unmasks enemy probing operations.
- **Hit:** A military attack launched against another kingdom.
- **Landgrab:** An attack intended to capture 5–10% of the target's land.
- **MA (Military Away):** When a kingdom has dispatched its army on an attack, leaving home defense depleted.
- **Monarch / SL:** Sector Leader, the leader of a sector who receives a +10% offensive and defensive bonus.
- **NAP:** Non-Aggression Pact between two kingdoms, sectors, or alliances.
- **NW / Networth:** Net Worth, the numerical measurement of a kingdom's overall strength and value.
- **OOD (Out of Defense):** A kingdom whose home military has been wiped or is away.
- **OOP (Out of Power):** A blackout state imposing a -20% combat penalty.
- **Overkill:** Sending far more offensive military power than needed to defeat the target's defense.
- **Retal (Retaliation):** Counter-attacking a kingdom that previously attacked you or a sector-mate.
- **Residenceless:** An advanced strategy where a player destroys all residences, replacing them with mines and barracks to eliminate civilian vulnerabilities.
- **SoK (Spy on Kingdom):** Probing operation that reveals resources, energy, buildings, and land.
- **SoM (Spy on Military):** Probing operation that reveals available home defense and unavailable away forces.
- **SoR (Spy on Research):** Probing operation that reveals enemy technology and research bonuses.
- **Tanks:** Heavy ground armor with 9 offense and 9 defense.
- **TF (Tactical Fighters):** Elite air units with 10 offense and 4 defense.
- **WL (War Leader):** Generals assigned to lead an attack force; sending multiple War Leaders staggers troop return times.
