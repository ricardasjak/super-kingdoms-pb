# Chapter 15: Social SK Skills

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/15_social.html`

Star Kingdoms is as much a political game as a military one. Strong interpersonal relations frequently protect kingdoms better than defensive walls.

## Sector & Alliance Diplomacy
- **Sector Unity:** A sector that works together, sharing intelligence and coordinating counter-attacks, will dominate fractured sectors.
- **Monarch Responsibilities:** The Sector Leader receives +10% offensive and defensive bonuses. In return, the Monarch coordinates sector defenses, sets diplomatic stances, and provides mutual aid.
- **Non-Aggression Pacts (NAPs):** Establishing formal NAPs with neighboring sectors prevents cross-border border skirmishes and avoids two-front wars.
