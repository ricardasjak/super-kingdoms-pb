# Chapter 6: Finding Targets

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/6_attacking.html`

Successful warfare in Star Kingdoms relies on patience and careful target selection. Attacking blindly is the fastest way to lose an army.

## Target Selection Criteria

### 1. Net Worth to Land Ratio (NW/Land)
Comparing a kingdom's total Net Worth against their land count reveals their military density:
- **Low Ratio (<120 NW/Land mid-game):** Likely under-defended, exploring rapidly without training troops, or heavily damaged by previous attacks. Prime target.
- **Moderate Ratio (150–220 NW/Land):** Average defended kingdom. Requires precise calculation via Spy on Military (SoM).
- **High Ratio (>280 NW/Land):** Heavy military fortress or elite tech kingdom. Unprofitable or dangerous to attack without overwhelming numbers.

### 2. Spotting Kingdoms with Armies Away
- Kingdoms that have recently attacked have portions of their military unavailable.
- An SoM report will reveal troops listed as `Unavail.(X)` (where X indicates hours until return).
- If a player sent their Tanks and Troopers on a 14-hour hit, their home defense drops dramatically for the next 14 hours. This is your window to strike.

### 3. Sector & Alliance Relations
- Always check the target's sector and alliance flags before launching:
  - **Yellow in sector list:** Sector Leader (+10% defense modifier).
  - **Peace Status:** Attacking a kingdom you have a peace treaty with gives them **+25% defense bonus**.
  - **Mutual Alliance War:** Attacks during declared alliance war receive **+25% offense bonus**.
