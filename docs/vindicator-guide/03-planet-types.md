# Chapter 3: Different Planet Types

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/3_planettypes.html`

Your choice of planet type defines your kingdom's persistent racial modifiers, economic strengths, and military biases.

## Planet Modifiers Summary

| Planet Type | Advantages | Disadvantages | Starting Tech | Recommended Playstyle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Volcanic Inferno** | -70% power consumption for units | -15% population | Fusion Technology | High unit count, military rush, late-game power efficiency |
| **Oceanic** | +20% defensive power | +10% research points needed | None | New players, pure defensive turtlers, landgrab deterrent |
| **Mystical Lands** | -30% attack travel time | -10% offensive power | Fusion Tech, S.C.O.U.T.E.R. | High-activity raiders, fast hit-and-run warfare |
| **Desert Wasteland** | +17.5% offensive power | -10% civilian population | None | Aggressive offensive players, heavy landgrab attackers |
| **Forest & Wilderness** | +15% population capacity | +15% attack travel time | None | Economy heavy, massive drafting pool, soldier spammers |
| **Mountainous** | +15% mining income, +15% probe defense | None | None | Probe specialists, economic mining builds, defensive hybrid |
| **Multiple Terrain** | +5% offense, +5% defense, +5% population | -5% mine income, +5% attack time | None | Balanced versatile players |
| **Terra Form** | Free initial choice / flexible conversion | Slight penalty during terraforming | Variable | Players adapting strategy dynamically |

## Detailed Breakdown

### Volcanic Inferno
- **Core Value:** Unit power maintenance is drastically reduced by 70%. In late game, standard kingdoms must dedicate 10–15% of all land to powerplants; Volcanic kingdoms can reallocate that land to barracks or mines.
- **Trade-off:** 15% lower population reduces tax revenue and daily drafting limits.

### Oceanic
- **Core Value:** +20% defense bonus is passive and active on all defensive units at home. Attackers actively avoid Oceanics early on because cracking their defense requires significantly more offense.
- **Trade-off:** +10% extra research points required for all techs is a minor penalty easily overcome with a few additional scientists.

### Mystical Lands
- **Core Value:** -30% attack time allows lightning attacks (returning troops much faster, reducing vulnerable downtime). Starts with both Fusion Tech and S.C.O.U.T.E.R. unlocked.
- **Trade-off:** -10% offensive power requires sending slightly more raw military to break targets.

### Desert Wasteland
- **Core Value:** +17.5% offense bonus makes Desert Wasteland the strongest raw breaker in the game. Breaches defensive walls that other kingdoms cannot touch.
- **Trade-off:** 10% lower population cap means fewer draftable civilians per residence.

### Forest and Wilderness
- **Core Value:** +15% population capacity allows massive civilian tax pools and the largest daily soldier draft in the universe.
- **Trade-off:** +15% longer attack travel times leave your army away and vulnerable for extended windows.

### Mountainous
- **Core Value:** Extra 15% income from Star Mines and +15% probe resistance against enemy espionage/sabotage operations.
