# Chapter 8: Using Your Probes

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/7_probing.html`

Probes are autonomous espionage and sabotage machines manufactured at Probe Factories. Having military strength without intelligence leads to failed attacks or wasteful troop commitments.

## Probe Operations

### 1. Espionage Missions
- **Spy on Military (SoM):** Essential before every attack. Reveals available home defense vs unavailable away troops.
- **Spy on Kingdom (SoK):** Reveals total money, current energy reserves, civilian count, building distribution, and power grid status.
- **Spy on Research (SoR):** Reveals the target's exact research levels (Military bonus %, shields, Warp Core, etc.).

### 2. Sabotage Missions
- **Rob Money:** Steals a percentage of the target's liquid treasury. Prime tool against economic "banks".
- **Kidnap Civilians:** Abducts civilian population to weaken their tax revenue and soldier drafting pool.
- **Arson Buildings:** Incinerates enemy residences, barracks, or powerplants. Devastating against residence-heavy economies.
- **Disable Shields:** Disrupts defensive shields before a major coordinated assault.
- **Assassinate Scientists:** Eliminates enemy research personnel to stall technological progression.

## Probe Mechanics & Defense
- **Probe Loss:** Probes sent on hostile missions risk interception and destruction based on the defender's probe defense.
- **Frequency Decryption Center (FDC):** Researching FDC (up to 25%) provides a percentage probability of identifying attackers even on successful enemy espionage runs.
- **Probe Shields:** Powering probe shields reduces enemy sabotage success rates and protects your intelligence from being gathered.
