# Chapter 1: What is Star Kingdoms?

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/1_intro.html`

For those who are completely new to Star Kingdoms and don't know anything at all about what it is or what they are supposed to do, here is a short description of the game.

## Overview
Star Kingdoms is a browser-based tick strategy game about war and diplomacy. You defend your own land while attacking other kingdoms. 

- **Landgrab Gain:** Every successful attack will award you **5% to 10% of the defender's land** (depending on how recently that kingdom was attacked prior to you).
- **Land Development:** Use newly captured land to construct buildings, which fuels military capacity and economic growth.
- **Population & Scaling:** A kingdom with 500 residences and 25,000 population will train twice as many soldiers and collect twice as much tax revenue as a kingdom with 250 residences and 12,500 population.

Star Kingdoms has no single fixed victory condition; players determine their own goals:
- Some strive to become **#1 in the Universe Net Worth / Land ranks**.
- More casual players aim to survive and defend their sector until the end of the round.
- Many participate for the intense social coordination, sector warfare, and alliance diplomacy.

## The Four Core Pillars of a Kingdom
Every kingdom is composed of four interconnected components that you must continually expand and protect:

1. **Military Force (Army):** Offensive units (Troopers, Dragoons, Tactical Fighters) and defensive units (Laser Troopers, Laser Dragoons, Tanks).
2. **Land & Infrastructure:** Captured land developed with specialized buildings (Residences, Barracks, Mines, Powerplants, Air Support Bays, Probe Factories).
3. **Civilians & Probes:** Civilians provide tax revenue and the manpower pool for soldier drafting; probes provide espionage, sabotage, and economic raiding.
4. **Economic Capital (Money & Power):** Money pays for construction, training, exploration, and research; power energizes your buildings, military machinery, and shields.
