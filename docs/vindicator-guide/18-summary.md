# Chapter 18: Summary of the Guide

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/21_summary.html`

A concise executive summary of the core gameplay rules, formulas, and strategic guidelines in Star Kingdoms.

## Summary Guidelines

### 1. Newbie Mode (First 72 Hours)
- Explore continually while exploration costs are low.
- Construct residences (35–45%), powerplants (10–15%), barracks (25–35%), and mines (5–15%).
- Draft up to **10% of civilians daily** into soldiers.
- Train at least 50–100 Scientists to research Fusion Tech (30,000 pts) and S.C.O.U.T.E.R. (15,000 pts).

### 2. Building & Economic Rules
- **Residences:** Capacity 50 base (60 max with research). Consume 0.35 power/hour. Population grows at max 2.5%/hour.
- **Star Mines:** Produce 120 money/hour. Immune to arson. Consume 0 power.
- **Taxes:** 1.95 money/hour per civilian.
- **Randomness:** ±5% hidden variance on hourly revenue.

### 3. Combat & Probing Rules
- **Landgrab:** Successful hits award 5% to 10% of defender land, civilians, power, and money.
- **Home Defense:** Always retain **50% to 70%** of total military at home.
- **Land Defense Minimum:** Defending kingdoms always have at least **2 * Land** defense points.
- **Attack Success:** `Total Offense >= Effective Defense + 1`. Ties result in failure.
- **Travel Time:** Governed by Euclidean distance on a 10x10 torus (`min(d, 10 - d)`).
