# Chapter 5: Offense and Defense

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/5_offdefense.html`

Star Kingdoms is fundamentally a game of warfare. Every combat interaction compares the attacker's total offensive power against the defender's home defense.

## Military Units Statistics

| Unit Type | Offense | Defense | Training Cost | Barracks Space | Tactical Role / Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Soldier** | 1 | 1 | 150 | 1 | Basic unit drafted from civilians (10%/day). Prerequisite for training all ground units. |
| **Trooper** | 4 | 0 | 350 | 1 | Pure offensive infantry. Cheap and cost-effective early attacker. |
| **Laser Trooper** | 0 | 4 | 400 | 1 | Pure defensive infantry. High defense-to-cost ratio. |
| **Dragoon** | 5 | 0 | 450 | 1 | Strong mobile attacker. Requires Dragoons research (60,000 pts). |
| **Laser Dragoon** | 0 | 5 | 550 | 1 | Heavy defensive wall unit. Requires Laser Dragoons research (72,000 pts). |
| **Tank** | 9 | 9 | 1,750 | 2 | Heavy dual-purpose unit (strong on both offense and defense). High NW density. |
| **Tactical Fighter** | 10 | 4 | 1,800 | 0 (Air Bay) | Elite air strike unit. Does not use barracks space. Requires AI Tech + Air Support Bays. |
| **Scientist** | 0 | 0 | 1,000 | 1 | Generates research points every hour. Does not fight. |

## The Home Defense Golden Rule
- Never leave your kingdom undefended when sending attacks.
- Standard survival guideline: Keep **50% to 70% of your total defense at home**.
- If you launch your entire army, opportunists will landgrab you while your troops are away, stripping away 5–10% of your land per hit.
