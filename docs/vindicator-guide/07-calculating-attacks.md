# Chapter 7: Calculating Attacks

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/7_calculating.html`

When attacking another kingdom, a successful hit captures between **5% and 10% of their land, money, power, and civilian population**. If your attack fails, your forces return defeated with zero gains, and your army is exposed.

Calculating attacks accurately ensures 100% success without wasting surplus forces (overkill) that should remain at home protecting your kingdom.

## Step 1: Obtain a Spy on Military (SoM) Report
Send probes to execute a **Spy on Military** operation. An SoM report displays:
- **Available Troops:** The units currently stationed at home that will defend against your attack.
- **Unavailable Troops `Unavail.(X)`:** Forces away on attacks, with the number in parentheses showing hours until they return.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Only count Available troops at home.** Troops that are away on mission contribute zero defense until they return.

## Step 2: Calculate Base Defense
Sum the defensive ratings of all units currently at home:

```
Base Defense = (Soldiers * 1) + (Laser Troopers * 4) + (Laser Dragoons * 5) + (Tanks * 9) + (Tactical Fighters * 4)
```

*(Note: Offensive Troopers and Dragoons provide 0 defense and are omitted from defense totals.)*

## Step 3: Calculate Defensive Modifiers
Defense modifiers alter the base defense as a percentage:

| Condition | Defense Modifier | How to Verify | Default Expectation (if unknown) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sector Leader (Monarch)** | +10% | Yellow highlight in sector list | Check sector list |
| **Oceanic Planet** | +20% | Inspect target planet type | Known from sector list |
| **Out of Power (Blackout)** | -20% | Spy on Kingdom (SoK) | Assume powered (0%) |
| **Peace Treaty with Attacker** | +25% | Check alliance/treaty status | 0% (unless treaty active) |
| **Military Research (MR)** | +1% per MR level | Spy on Research (SoR) | Assume +30% (max research) |

```
Defensive Bonus Multiplier (%) = 100 + PlanetMod + AllianceMod + MilitaryResearch% + SectorLeaderMod - PowerPenalty
```

```
Total Modified Defense = (Base Defense * Defensive Bonus Multiplier) / 100
```

## Step 4: The Minimum Land Defense Rule
A defending kingdom always possesses a baseline structural defense generated by their land:

```
Land Defense = Defender Land * 2
```

> [!CAUTION]
> If `Total Modified Defense < Land Defense`, the actual defense is raised to `Land Defense`.  
> A kingdom with 2,000 land will **never** have less than 4,000 defense points, even if they have zero military units stationed at home!

```
Effective Defense = max(Total Modified Defense, Land Defense)
```

## Step 5: Calculate Required Offense
Sum the offensive power of the attacking army you plan to send:

```
Base Offense = (Soldiers * 1) + (Troopers * 4) + (Dragoons * 5) + (Tanks * 9) + (Tactical Fighters * 10)
```

Apply attacker offensive modifiers:
- **Alliance War:** +25% (if mutual war declared).
- **Planet Modifiers:** Desert Wasteland (+17.5%), Volcanic (+10%), Mystical Lands (-10%).
- **Military Research:** +1% per MR level (up to +30%).
- **War Leaders:** +1% per War Leader sent (or bonus per WL level).
- **Attacker Sector Leader:** +10% if you are the Monarch.
- **Attacker Power Blackout:** -20% penalty if you are out of power.
- **Defender Shields:** -1% per active defender shield (up to -10%).

```
Total Modified Offense = (Base Offense * Offensive Bonus Multiplier) / 100
```

## Victory Condition
```
Attack Succeeds IF: Total Modified Offense >= Effective Defense + 1
```
*(Ties result in attack failure. You must exceed the defense by at least 1 point to win.)*
