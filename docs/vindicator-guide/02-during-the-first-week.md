# Chapter 2: During the First Week

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/2_firstweek.html`

The first weeks of the game are the most critical in Star Kingdoms. Early pacing determines how formidable your position will be for the remainder of the round. Having twice the strength of your peers after week one establishes momentum that lasts through end-game.

## Newbie Protection (First 72 Hours)
When a round begins or a new kingdom registers, it enters **Newbie Protection** for 72 hours (3 real days):
- You cannot attack or probe other kingdoms.
- Other kingdoms cannot attack or probe you.
- Use this window exclusively to explore, construct infrastructure, establish power security, draft civilians, and kickstart research.

### Step 1: Exploration
- Continually explore as much land as your economy allows early on.
- As your land count rises, exploration costs scale up. Early exploration is the cheapest land you will acquire before combat opens up.

### Step 2: Build Order & Ratios
A reliable baseline distribution for the first 72 hours:
- **Residences (35–45%):** Needed for population growth, tax revenue, and soldier drafting capacity.
- **Powerplants (10–15%):** Nuclear early, transitioning to Fusion once researched. Never fall into power deficit.
- **Barracks (25–35%):** Required to house your drafted military forces so they do not displace civilians from residences.
- **Star Mines (5–15%):** Instant, reliable revenue that requires zero power and cannot be arsoned.
- **Probe Factories & Air Support Bays:** Build later once basic infrastructure stabilizes.

### Step 3: Soldier Drafting & Training
- **Daily Draft Limit:** You can draft up to **10% of your civilian population** into soldiers every 24 hours.
- Draft soldiers every day without fail. If you don't draft, that military potential is permanently delayed.
- Once drafted, train soldiers into specialized units according to your planet type and strategy:
  - **Offensive Units:** Troopers, Dragoons.
  - **Defensive Units:** Laser Troopers, Laser Dragoons, Tanks.

### Step 4: Early Research
- Train at least **50–100 Scientists** during Newbie Mode.
- Priority early research projects:
  1. **Fusion Technology (30,000 pts):** Unlocks efficient Fusion Powerplants.
  2. **S.C.O.U.T.E.R. (15,000 pts):** Displays distance/attack travel times for all potential targets.
  3. **Dragoons / Laser Dragoons:** Core offensive and defensive units.
  4. **Population Bonus (Land * Land * 0.01):** Increases residence capacity up to +20%.

## Exiting Newbie Mode
Before your 72-hour timer expires:
- Ensure your kingdom is not left defenseless with all units untrained.
- Retain at least 50–70% of your military power at home to deter immediate predator hits.
- Scout your local sector and neighboring sectors for uncoordinated or dormant kingdoms.
