# Chapter 10: Power and Shields

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/10_power.html`

Energy is the operational lifeblood of your kingdom. Power is generated by Nuclear and Fusion Powerplants and consumed by civilian populations, military units, and defensive shielding.

## Power Generation vs Consumption
- **Civilians:** Each civilian consumes **0.35 power/hour**.
- **Military Units:** High-tech units (Laser Dragoons, Tanks, Tactical Fighters) have ongoing power maintenance requirements.
- **Volcanic Inferno Advantage:** Reduces all military unit power consumption by **70%**.

## The Blackout Penalty (Out of Power)
If total power consumption exceeds generation and energy reserves drop to zero:
1. **Military Degradation:** Both offensive and defensive armies suffer an immediate **-20% combat penalty**.
2. **Residence Capacity Collapse:** Overcrowding occurs, causing civilian mortality.
3. **Population Growth Stagnation:** Civilian birth rate drops close to 0%.

## Defensive Shields
- Kingdoms can allocate surplus power to activate up to **10 defensive shields**.
- **Combat Effect:** Each active shield reduces incoming attack power by **1%** (up to a maximum **-10% offensive penalty** to any attacker).
- **Maintenance Cost:** Maintaining 10 active shields consumes substantial hourly power, requiring additional powerplants.
