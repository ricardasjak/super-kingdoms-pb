# Chapter 4: The Buildings

> **Source:** Unofficial Star Kingdoms Guide by Vindicator (v2, 2003)  
> **Original URL:** `http://www.angelfire.com/ill/l33t_hacker/Vindicator/4_buildings.html`

Star Kingdoms features eight distinct building types. Proper land allocation is fundamental to sustained growth.

## The Eight Buildings

### 1. Residences
- **Capacity:** Houses **50 civilians** base, increasing up to **60 civilians** with full Population Research (+20%).
- **Economic Value:** Civilians generate tax revenue every tick and form the 10% daily soldier drafting pool.
- **Power Usage:** Civilians consume **0.35 power/hour** each.
- **Growth Rate:** Population grows organically by up to **2.5% per hour** when residences have free capacity.
- **Vulnerability:** Highly vulnerable to enemy arson and kidnapping ops. If arsoned, lost residences cause overcrowding.

### 2. Barracks
- **Capacity:** Houses **75 military units** per barrack (Tanks require **2 spaces** each). Tactical Fighters and Probes do not occupy barrack space.
- **Overcrowding Rule:** If you lack sufficient barracks for your troops, military units displace civilians from residences, severely damaging tax income and civilian growth.

### 3. Star Mines
- **Revenue:** Generates **120 money/hour** baseline.
- **Power Usage:** Requires **0 power**.
- **Net Worth & Arson:** Adds less Net Worth than fully populated residences, but **cannot be arsoned or destroyed**. Immune to enemy probe sabotage.

### 4. Nuclear Powerplants
- **Output:** Basic early-game energy generation.
- **Usage:** Essential during the first 72 hours before Fusion Technology is researched. Eventually dismantled to make room for more efficient Fusion plants.

### 5. Fusion Powerplants
- **Requirement:** Requires **Fusion Technology (30,000 research points)**.
- **Output:** Produces significantly more power per acre than nuclear plants. Powers heavy military units, shields, and civilian grids.

### 6. Training Camps
- **Function:** Accelerates military unit training times and reduces soldier training costs.
- **Strategic Use:** Critical during wartime when rapid troop replenishment between hits is necessary.

### 7. Air Support Bays
- **Requirement:** Requires **Vesudian Core and AI Technology (600,000 research points)**.
- **Capacity:** Houses Tactical Fighters (which do not consume standard barrack space).

### 8. Probe Factories
- **Function:** Manufactures Probes every hour based on factory count and funding.
- **Strategic Use:** Powers intelligence gathering (SoM, SoK, SoR) and sabotage operations (arson, theft, shield disruption).
