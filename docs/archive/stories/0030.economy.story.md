## Kingdom's economy

Each tick a kingdom is growing:
- gold income (population * 2 + star mines * 120) * [sum of economy bonuses]
- energy production (power plants * 140) * [sum of power production bonuses]
- net energy:  energy production - total energy consumption
- population target = residences * 50 * [sum of population bonuses] - [75* (sum of barracks space deficit)/50]
- population change: current population can drop by 5 perc or grow by 2.5 perc per tick, aiming for population target
- kingdom cannot go negative on power or population
- kingdom at 0 population enters `kd-dead` mode
- kingdom at 0 energy, loses 20% population and 20% military strength

- kingdom has no limit how much gold it can stockpile
- kingdom has energy storage limit: power plants x 1000. Any energy surplus above that limit is lost.
