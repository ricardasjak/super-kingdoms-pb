## Missiles

Missiles provide instantaneous long-range strikes against rival kingdoms without committing military units or war leaders.

### Missile types

| Missile | Money Cost | Power Cost | Land Destroyed | Population % Destroyed | Power % Destroyed | Building % Destroyed | Max Buildings Destroyed | Land % Destroyed | Max Land Destroyed |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Nuclear | $100,000 | 50,000 | 50 | 2.5% | 0% | 0.00% | 0 | 0.00% | 50 |
| Fusion | $500,000 | 100,000 | 100 | 5.0% | 5% | 0.00% | 0 | 0.00% | 100 |
| Energy | $1,000,000 | 200,000 | 200 | 5.0% | 10% | 0.00% | 0 | 0.00% | 200 |

### Rules

- Launching requires money and power, deducted immediately on launch.
- Detonation instantly destroys target land, population, and stored energy.
- Buildings are not damaged (0% destroyed).
- Target's **Missile Shield** provides up to 100% interception chance (see `0050.shields.story.md`). Intercepted missiles deal 0 damage.
