## Exploration Mechanics

Exploration expands a kingdom's territory by sending scouts into uncharted territory to discover new land. Acquired land can subsequently be developed with buildings, populated, and taxed.

Exploration is managed entirely through the **Exploration Rate (Level 0–10)** setting; there is no manual one-off exploration dispatch.

---

### Core Mechanics & Limits

- **Duration / Pipeline**: Exploration runs over a **24-tick (24-hour)** pipeline. Newly discovered land is distributed across a 24-tick queue (`landQueue`) and arrives incrementally each tick.
- **Exploration Ceiling**: The active exploration pipeline is capped at **10% of current kingdom land**.
  $$\text{Max Pipeline} = \lfloor \text{land} \times 0.10 \rfloor$$
- **Tick-Based Scouting**: On each tick, if exploration is active (> Level 0), the kingdom automatically scouts land up to its configured target rate, provided sufficient gold is available.
- **Batching**: To distribute incoming land evenly across all 24 hours of the pipeline, dispatches occur in multiples of 24 acres (so at least 1 acre arrives each hour).

---

### Base Exploration Cost

Base cost per acre scales with kingdom size via a square-root formula:

$$\text{Base Cost} = \sqrt{\text{land}} \times 111 \text{ gold}$$

---

### Exploration Levels & Escalating Cost Multipliers

Kingdoms control exploration pace via an exploration level from **Level 0 (Off)** to **Level 10 (Maximum)**.

Each level governs two parameters:
1. **Target Pipeline Capacity**: Targets maintaining an active pipeline equal to **$L\%$** of current land ($\text{limitPct} = L \times 0.01$).
2. **Level Cost Multiplier**: Higher levels prioritize rapid territorial acquisition over scouting efficiency, increasing the gold cost per acre.

$$\text{Cost per Acre} = \text{round}\left(\text{Base Cost} \times \text{LevelMultiplier} \times \text{LandMultiplier}\right)$$

#### Level Multipliers (Levels 1–10)

| Level | Target Pipeline (% of Land) | Cost Multiplier | Relative Cost vs. Base | Scouting Pace & Role |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| **0** | 0% | — | Off | Exploration disabled |
| **1** | 1% | **0.165×** | -83.5% (Heavy discount) | Minimal trickle, extreme cost efficiency |
| **2** | 2% | **0.330×** | -67.0% discount | Steady conservative expansion |
| **3** | 3% | **0.500×** | -50.0% discount | Half-price steady scouting |
| **4** | 4% | **0.670×** | -33.0% discount | Moderate growth |
| **5** | 5% | **0.835×** | -16.5% discount | Balanced sustainable growth |
| **6** | 6% | **1.000×** | Standard (100% of Base) | Standard exploration cost baseline |
| **7** | 7% | **1.250×** | +25% surcharge | Aggressive expansion |
| **8** | 8% | **1.500×** | +50% surcharge | High-speed land grab |
| **9** | 9% | **1.750×** | +75% surcharge | Rush scouting |
| **10** | 10% | **2.000×** | +100% surcharge (Double cost) | Maximum rush, highest gold expenditure |

---

### Early Kingdom Land Size Multipliers

To assist smaller and developing kingdoms, an additional progressive discount applies based on total land:

| Kingdom Land | Land Size Multiplier | Discount Effect |
|---|:---:|---|
| **< 1,000 acres** | **0.25×** | 75% early-game discount |
| **1,000 – 2,499 acres** | **0.50×** | 50% discount |
| **2,500 – 4,999 acres** | **0.75×** | 25% discount |
| **5,000+ acres** | **1.00×** | Full standard rate |

---

### Example Cost Comparison

For a kingdom with **2,500 acres** ($\text{Base Cost} = \sqrt{2500} \times 111 = 50 \times 111 = 5,550$ gold per acre, $\text{LandMultiplier} = 0.75$):

- **Level 1**: $5,550 \times 0.165 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{687}$ gold / acre
- **Level 3**: $5,550 \times 0.500 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{2,081}$ gold / acre
- **Level 6**: $5,550 \times 1.000 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{4,163}$ gold / acre
- **Level 10**: $5,550 \times 2.000 \times 0.75 = \mathbf{8,325}$ gold / acre (~12× more expensive than Level 1)
