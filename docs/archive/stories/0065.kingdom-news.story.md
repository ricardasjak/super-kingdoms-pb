## Kingdom News (Kingdom News Room)

The Kingdom News Room (`KingdomNews.aspx`) is the kingdom's centralized event log and tactical feed. It maintains a historical record of all military actions, defense engagements, missile strikes, and covert hostile interactions involving the player's kingdom.

It serves as the critical feedback loop for combat: notifying players of attacks received while offline, tracking outbound campaign results, and enabling coordinated retaliation with alliance members.

---

### Page Layout and Navigation

- **Header Banner:** The page displays a stylized `Kingdom News Room` header.
- **Top Navigation Indicator:** Unread notifications or incoming attacks can illuminate or increment the notification bell on the game's top status bar.

---

### Time Window and Filtering Controls

Players can filter the event feed by time window and event category:

1. **Date Range Dropdown (`cboDate`):**
   - Options:
     - `today` (default)
     - `the last 3 days`
     - `the last 7 days`
     - `the last 30 days`
   - Accompanied by a `View` button to fetch events matching the selected window.

2. **Category Filter Tabs:**
   - **All (`N` items):** Aggregated chronological view of every event involving the kingdom.
   - **Attacks (`N` items):** Outbound military expeditions launched by the player against foreign kingdoms.
   - **Defends (`N` items):** Inbound attacks launched by other players or AI raiders against this kingdom.
   - **Missiles (`N` items):** Missile strikes (both outbound launches and inbound impacts).

---

### News Item Structure

Each news item represents a discrete combat or tactical event formatted as a structured row:

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Checkbox** | Checkbox for individual selection (`Select this report`), alongside a master `Select all shown news` checkbox in the table header. Used with the **Copy News** utility. |
| **Category Badge** | Distinct visual pill/badge indicating event type (e.g. `🎯 ATTACK` in amber, `🛡️ DEFEND` in blue, `🚀 MISSILE` in purple). |
| **Timestamp** | Date and time of the event (e.g. `Oct 28, 1:16 PM`) and corresponding game tick. |
| **Narrative Report** | Descriptive summary detailing combatants, sector coordinates, and outcomes: |
| | - Participating kingdoms with sector coordinates: e.g. `WOLOLO (1:9)` and `Kesha (1:1)`. |
| | - Kingdom names are interactive hyperlinks linking to the target's `Profile.aspx?KingdomID=<id>`. |
| | - Conquered or lost land: e.g. `conquered 🌐 20 land`. |
| | - Casualties, plundered resources (money/power), or damaged buildings. |

#### Examples of News Reports

- **Successful Outbound Attack:**
  > `[ATTACK] Oct 28, 1:16 PM` — *Our brave kingdom WOLOLO (1:9) attacked Kesha (1:1) and conquered 🌐 20 land.*
- **Failed Outbound Attack:**
  > `[ATTACK] Oct 27, 4:20 PM` — *Our forces attacked Cracker (1:9) but were repelled by their planetary defenses. We lost 120 Laser Troopers and 40 Heavy Tanks.*
- **Inbound Attack (Defended):**
  > `[DEFEND] Oct 26, 8:15 AM` — *The enemy forces of RaiderBot (1:9) assaulted our borders, but our stationed defense fleet successfully repelled the invaders.*
- **Inbound Attack (Breached):**
  > `[DEFEND] Oct 25, 11:02 PM` — *DarkLegion (1:4) attacked our kingdom and conquered 🌐 85 land. 14 residences and 6 power plants were destroyed.*
- **Missile Strike:**
  > `[MISSILE] Oct 25, 9:30 PM` — *A nuclear missile launched by Goliath (1:2) impacted our territory, destroying 42,000 power and killing 1,200 population.*

---

### Utilities and Social Integration

1. **Copy News Button:**
   - Positioned above the table (`Copy News` button with copy icon).
   - Clicking copies all selected reports (or all currently displayed reports if none are explicitly selected) to the user's system clipboard.
   - Formatted cleanly as a text/markdown block suitable for instant pasting into Discord, alliance war rooms, or community message boards for battle reporting and tactical calls.

2. **Profile & Retaliation Integration:**
   - Clicking any enemy kingdom mentioned in a news report navigates directly to their `Profile.aspx`.
   - From their profile (or by looking up their sector coordinates in Sector View), players can initiate counter-probes (`Probe.aspx`), retaliatory military strikes (`Attack.aspx`), or launch missiles (`Missile.aspx`).

### Open Questions & System Considerations

- **Event Retention Policy:** How long are events stored in the database? (e.g. maximum 30 days or round duration, with automatic pruning of older records to prevent excessive database growth).
- **Probes / Espionage Logging:** Are detected enemy espionage attempts (e.g. failed robbery, caught arson probes) displayed under Kingdom News or in a dedicated Spy/Probe History room?
- **Detailed Battle Reports:** Does clicking an attack entry open a full tactical battle report breakdown (offense vs defense breakdown, War Leader assignments, precise round-by-round casualties), or is the summary sentence sufficient for the news feed?
