## Newbie mode: Server state & kingdom mode

Newbie mode operates on two distinct levels: the **Server Game State** (`newbiemode`) and an individual kingdom's **Kingdom Mode** (`kd-newbiemode`).

---

### 1. Server game state: `newbiemode`

- **Purpose:** The round warmup and registration window for the entire game universe.
- **Trigger:** Initiated automatically as soon as a new round begins.
- **Duration:** Lasts at least **72 hours** (configurable) and always concludes at **16:00 UTC**.
- **Server Clock:** **No global server ticks occur.** The universe clock remains frozen so early registrants do not gain passive tick advantages over players who register later during the window.
- **Protection:** All player kingdoms are completely invulnerable; no combat or espionage can take place.
- **Transition:** Once the server timer expires at 16:00 UTC, the server state advances to `pve` mode.

---

### 2. Kingdom mode: `kd-newbiemode`

Every newly created kingdom enters `kd-newbiemode`. It is a self-paced, guided opening build and tutorial phase.

#### Step progression & self-ticking
- **8 Discrete Steps:** Instead of tedious individual ticks, onboarding is broken down into **8 milestone steps**.
- **16 Ticks Per Step:** Triggering a step button executes **16 game ticks** of simulation at once ($8 \times 16 = 128$ total ticks of development).
- **Manual Agency:** Players advance at their own pace. The auto-builder is disabled so rulers learn hands-on construction, scientist hiring, and military drafting.

#### Safety & free resets
- **Complete Isolation:** Kingdoms cannot attack and cannot be targeted by any other kingdom or bot.
- **Free Resets:** Players can reset their kingdom back to creation state (with a new race and planet type) at any point during newbie mode, and **even upon graduation if the server is still in `newbiemode`**. This enables players to test and practice different opening strategies as many times as they wish before the universe launches.
- **Graduation Snapshot & Rollback:** When a kingdom successfully graduates (completes Step 8), the game automatically saves a snapshot of that graduated kingdom state. If the player chooses to reset and test a new opening, they can **roll back to their previously graduated kingdom** at any time while the server remains in `newbiemode`, ensuring experimentation is completely risk-free.

### 3. Step progression & cumulative milestones (Steps 1–8)

To teach essential survival and growth mechanics, avoid common newcomer traps, and prevent economic regressions, the game enforces **cumulative milestone requirements**. 

Advancing to any step requires satisfying the criteria for that step **plus all previous steps combined** ($\text{Requirements for Step } N = \text{Criteria } 1 \cup \dots \cup \text{Criteria } N$):
- **Compounding Health Check:** A player cannot sacrifice earlier achievements to satisfy later steps. For example, exploring new land in later steps still requires that land to be built (Step 1 rule), and buying troops or high-power buildings cannot drop the energy balance below +2,000 (Step 6 rule) or cause barracks overcrowding (Step 5 rule).
- If any active or previously unlocked requirement is violated, the step advance button is disabled with an actionable checklist showing which criteria are failing.

| Step | Newly Introduced Requirement | Cumulative Requirements Checked | Goal / Learning Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Step 1** | **All free land must be built** (buildings in construction count) | Step 1 | Teaches land development; ensures no acreage sits idle. |
| **Step 2** | **All scientists must be assigned** | Steps 1–2 | Teaches research prioritization; ensures scientific points are allocated. |
| **Step 3** | **At least 400 scientists bought** ($\text{sci} \ge 400$) | Steps 1–3 | Establishes early technological momentum and research velocity. |
| **Step 4** | **Housing capacity sufficient for population** ($\text{resCap} \ge \text{pop}$) | Steps 1–4 | Teaches housing management; prevents civilian crowding penalties. |
| **Step 5** | **Barracks capacity at 100% max** ($\text{raxCap} \ge \text{raxUsage}$) | Steps 1–5 | Ensures military housing covers forces, preventing civilian displacement. |
| **Step 6** | **Power balance (net income) $\ge 2{,}000$ energy/tick** | Steps 1–6 | Establishes strong energy surplus for high-tech units and structures. |
| **Step 7** | **Have at least 10% Training Camps of current land** | Steps 1–7 | Teaches military production efficiency; locks in max ~30% training discount. |
| **Step 8** | **At least 8,000 base defense points** (active + queued units) | Steps 1–8 (All) | Ensures the kingdom is fortified before entering hostile PvE/PvP stages. |

#### Detailed criteria rules (Cumulative Checklist)

- **Criteria 1 — Zero Undeveloped Land:**
  $$\text{Free Land} = \text{total land} - \left(\sum\text{built} + \sum\text{queued} + \text{rubble}\right) = 0$$
  All available land (including land acquired from auto-exploration in later steps) must have buildings queued or completed.
- **Criteria 2 — Research Allocation:**
  All research points produced by scientists must be actively assigned (via manual allocation or `researchAutoAssign` priorities) rather than accumulating unspent.
- **Criteria 3 — Scientific Core:**
  Total scientists must remain at least **400** ($\text{military.sci} \ge 400$), commissioned from the research lab.
- **Criteria 4 — Housing Adequacy:**
  Total residential capacity (including residences under construction) must equal or exceed current population ($\text{resCap} \ge \text{population}$), guaranteeing adequate living space for civilian growth.
- **Criteria 5 — Barracks Sufficiency:**
  Total barracks capacity (active plus queued $\times 75$) must cover 100% of ground military housing demand ($\text{raxCap} \ge \text{raxUsage}$), eliminating barracks overcrowding penalties.
- **Criteria 6 — Energy Surplus:**
  Net power income per tick must remain at least **$+2{,}000$ energy/tick** ($\text{powerProduction} - \text{powerConsumption} \ge 2{,}000$).
- **Criteria 7 — Military Cost Optimization:**
  Total Training Camps (active + queued) must represent at least **10% of total land** ($(\text{tc} / \text{land}) \ge 0.10$), maintaining maximum military training discounts.
- **Criteria 8 — Fortress Readiness Gate:**
  The sum of intrinsic base defense values across standing units and units in the training queue must be at least **8,000 points** before modifiers:
  $$\sum_{\text{unit } u} \left[ (\text{activeCount}_u + \text{queuedCount}_u) \times \text{def}_u \right] \ge 8{,}000$$

---

### 4. Completion & transitioning to PvE
- **If Server is still in `newbiemode`:** Kingdoms that complete Step 8 reach graduation and wait safely in holding (with their snapshot recorded). Players can keep their finalized build, reset to test alternative builds, or **roll back to their saved graduated build** at any time while the warmup window remains active.
- **If Server is already in `pve` or `pvp`:** Completing Step 8 immediately transitions the kingdom to `kd-pvemode` to begin PvE bot combat and catch-up ticking (resetting and rollbacks are locked once graduated into an active server phase).
