## Planetary Defense Shields

Planetary shields are high-energy defense grids deployed across a kingdom's territory. They provide active protection against hostile military invasions, covert espionage probes, and long-range missile strikes.

Shield grids consume continuous electrical power each tick. Their upkeep scales directly with total kingdom territory (**land in acres**) and the ruler's configured shield allocation level.

---

### The Three Shield Systems

| Shield System | Maximum Protection | Full Upkeep (Power / Acre) | Primary Protective Effect |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Military Shield** | **+10%** Defense | **2.5** power / land | Fortifies military defensive potential during combat |
| **Probe Shield** | **-25%** Enemy Success Ratio | **3.5** power / land | Scrambles hostile reconnaissance and sabotage probes |
| **Missile Shield** | **100%** Interception | **1.9** power / land | Blocks incoming missile bombardments and warheads |

---

### Mechanics & Formulas

Rulers can configure each shield grid independently from **0% (Disabled)** to **100% (Maximum Power)** via operational power sliders.

#### 1. Military Shield
- **Effect**: Bolsters the kingdom's total defensive power calculation during an enemy attack.
- **Max Benefit**: Up to **+10%** bonus to kingdom defense.
- **Power Drain**: **2.5 power per acre of land** at 100% capacity.
- **Formulas**:
  $$\text{Defense Bonus \%} = 10\% \times \left(\frac{\text{Military Shield \%}}{100}\right)$$
  $$\text{Power Upkeep} = 2.5 \times \text{land} \times \left(\frac{\text{Military Shield \%}}{100}\right)$$

#### 2. Probe Shield
- **Effect**: Emits electromagnetic jamming and sensor distortion to counter incoming covert operations (e.g. State of Kingdom surveillance, sabotage).
- **Max Benefit**: Reduces enemy probe operation success ratio by up to **25%**.
- **Power Drain**: **3.5 power per acre of land** at 100% capacity.
- **Formulas**:
  $$\text{Enemy Success Reduction \%} = 25\% \times \left(\frac{\text{Probe Shield \%}}{100}\right)$$
  $$\text{Power Upkeep} = 3.5 \times \text{land} \times \left(\frac{\text{Probe Shield \%}}{100}\right)$$

#### 3. Missile Shield
- **Effect**: Deploys orbital interception batteries and directed-energy counter-measures against incoming ballistic, orbital, or tactical missiles.
- **Max Benefit**: Intercepts up to **100%** of incoming missiles (full protection against missile bombardment).
- **Power Drain**: **1.9 power per acre of land** at 100% capacity.
- **Formulas**:
  $$\text{Missile Interception \%} = 100\% \times \left(\frac{\text{Missile Shield \%}}{100}\right)$$
  $$\text{Power Upkeep} = 1.9 \times \text{land} \times \left(\frac{\text{Missile Shield \%}}{100}\right)$$

---

### Energy Grid & Upkeep Rules

1. **Total Shield Upkeep**:
   On each tick, total power consumed by active shields is calculated as:
   $$\text{Total Shield Power} = \text{land} \times \left(2.5 \times \frac{\text{Mil\%}}{100} + 3.5 \times \frac{\text{Probe\%}}{100} + 1.9 \times \frac{\text{Missile\%}}{100}\right)$$

2. **Maximum Combined Load**:
   If all three shields are fully energized at 100%, the kingdom draws:
   $$2.5 + 3.5 + 1.9 = \mathbf{7.9 \text{ power per acre of land / tick}}$$

3. **Power Blackout & Grid Failure**:
   - Shields require sustained power from Power Plants (`plants`) or battery reserves.
   - If energy reserves hit **0** and net energy generation is negative (energy blackout), shields cannot maintain containment fields and immediately **collapse (0% effect)** for that tick until energy balance is restored.

### Example Scenarios

#### Example A: Balanced Frontier Kingdom (1,000 Acres)
- **Settings**: Military: 100%, Probe: 50%, Missile: 50%
- **Protection**:
  - Defense bonus: $+10\%$
  - Probe jamming: $-12.5\%$ enemy success ratio
  - Missile interception: $50\%$
- **Power Upkeep**:
  - Military: $1,000 \times 2.5 \times 1.0 = 2,500$ power/tick
  - Probe: $1,000 \times 3.5 \times 0.5 = 1,750$ power/tick
  - Missile: $1,000 \times 1.9 \times 0.5 = 950$ power/tick
  - **Total**: $5,200$ power/tick (requires ~52 base Power Plants without tech bonuses).

#### Example B: Full Shield Lockdown (2,500 Acres)
- **Settings**: Military: 100%, Probe: 100%, Missile: 100%
- **Protection**: $+10\%$ defense, $-25\%$ enemy probe success, $100\%$ missile immunity.
- **Power Upkeep**: $2,500 \times 7.9 = \mathbf{19,750\text{ power/tick}}$.
