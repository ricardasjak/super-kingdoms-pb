## In-Game Player Messaging

The In-Game Messaging system (`Msg.aspx`, `SendMessages.aspx`, `Sent.aspx`) provides private, asynchronous communication between players and kingdoms. It enables diplomatic coordination, trade arrangements, truce discussions, and direct player-to-player correspondence across the galaxy.

---

### Navigation & Access

1. **Top Bar Status Indicator:**
   - A persistent mail / envelope icon on the top navigation bar displays an unread message badge count (e.g. `✉ (2)`).
   - Clicking opens the messaging center or links directly to `Msg.aspx`.
2. **Tabbed Sub-Navigation:**
   - **Read Messages / Inbox (`Msg.aspx`):** View received messages from other kingdoms or system notices.
   - **Send Message / Compose (`SendMessages.aspx`):** Compose and dispatch outbound messages.
   - **Sent Messages (`Sent.aspx`):** History log of outbound messages sent by the kingdom.

---

### Inbox (`Msg.aspx`)

The inbox displays private correspondence received by the active kingdom:

- **Message Retention & Expiry:**
  - Messages are automatically deleted after **3 days** (72 hours / round equivalent) to prevent database bloat and ensure relevant active communications.
  - A prominent notice informs players: *"Messages are automatically deleted after 3 days. Please save any important messages elsewhere."*
- **Message Item Structure:**
  - **Header:** Sender kingdom name, ruler title, and galactic coordinates `Galaxy:Sector` (e.g. `High Archon of Avalon (1:4)`).
  - **Timestamp:** Exact date/time and game tick when the message was delivered.
  - **Subject:** Thread topic or message subject header.
  - **Body:** Message narrative text.
- **Actions per Message:**
  - **Reply:** Pre-populates `SendMessages.aspx` with recipient coordinates and `Re: <Subject>`.
  - **Delete:** Permanently removes the message from the kingdom's inbox.
  - **Block / Mute (Optional):** Prevents harassment from hostile or spam accounts.

---

### Compose & Dispatch (`SendMessages.aspx`)

The compose interface allows players to send direct messages to any valid kingdom in the universe:

- **Target Addressing Options:**
  - **Galactic Coordinates:** Direct sector and kingdom number entry (e.g. `Sector: [ 1 ] Kingdom: [ 4 ]`).
  - **Quick-Fill from Profile/Sector:** Clicking the message icon next to any kingdom in the [Sector View](0064.sector-view.story.md) or Target Finder automatically opens compose with their coordinates pre-filled.
- **Form Fields:**
  - **Recipient:** Target sector and kingdom identifier.
  - **Subject:** Text field (max 100 characters).
  - **Message Body:** Multiline text area supporting plain text formatting.
  - **Send Button:** Validates recipient existence before dispatching.
- **Rules & Restrictions:**
  - Players cannot send messages to their own kingdom.
  - Messages cannot be sent to deleted or non-existent kingdoms.
  - Basic rate-limiting to prevent automated spamming.

---

### Sent Log (`Sent.aspx`)

- Displays outbound messages sent by the player within the 3-day retention window.
- Shows recipient coordinates, subject, dispatch timestamp, and message body.
