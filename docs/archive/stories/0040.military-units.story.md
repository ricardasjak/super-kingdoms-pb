## Military units & armed forces

The Armed Forces Deck allows rulers to draft base soldiers, train specialized ground divisions, construct aerospace strike fleets, deploy heavy armor, and commission research scientists. Military units defend the kingdom against hostile invasions and project offensive power across the realm.

### Recruitment & training mechanics

#### 1. Soldiers (`sol`) — The foundation of the army
- **Drafting:** Soldiers are enlisted from the civilian population.
- **Draft Limit:** A kingdom cannot train more soldiers at once than **10% of its current population**:
  $$\text{Max Soldiers in Queue} = \lfloor\text{population} \times 0.10\rfloor$$
- **Training Duration:** **16 ticks** (faster than specialized units).
- **Function:** In addition to providing baseline 1/1 combat strength, unassigned soldiers (`military.sol`) are consumed when training specialized ground combatants and armored vehicles.

#### 2. Specialized combatants & vehicles
- **Training Duration:** **24 ticks** across all specialized units (`tr`, `dr`, `ft`, `lt`, `ld`, `lf`, `f74`, `tf`, `t`, `ht`).
- **Personnel Cost:** Ground infantry and tanks consume **1 unassigned soldier recruit** per unit trained.
- **Unmanned / Aerospace Fleet:** F-74 Drones and Tactical Fighters are manufactured craft and require **0 soldiers**.
- **Queue Pipeline:** Enqueued units are distributed evenly across the training duration:
  $$\text{Units per tick} = \lfloor\text{amount} / \text{duration}\rfloor$$
  Remainder units are added to the later ticks of the pipeline. On each tick, the front of the queue finishes training and joins the active army.

#### 3. Scientists (`sci`)
- Commissioned for 1,000 gold.
- Produce 1 research point per tick toward scientific endeavors.
- Housed in Barracks and consume 0.7 power per tick like ground personnel.

### Training camp discounts

Constructing Training Camps (`tc`) reduces the gold training cost of combat units by up to **30%**:

$$\text{TC Discount \%} = \min\left(30, \left\lfloor \frac{\text{tc}}{\text{land}} \times 300 \right\rfloor\right)$$

$$\text{Discounted Cost} = \max\left(1, \text{round}\left(\text{base cost} \times \left(1 - \frac{\text{TC Discount}}{100}\right)\right)\right)$$

- **Applies to:** Troopers, Dragoons, Fighters, Tactical Fighters, Laser Troopers, Laser Dragoons, Laser Fighters, F-74 Drones, Tanks, and Hover Tanks.
- **Does not apply to:** Soldiers (`sol`, always 150 gold) or Scientists (`sci`, always 1,000 gold).

### Housing & capacity rules

Military forces require dedicated facilities. Inadequate housing creates severe penalties or prevents training altogether:

1. **Barracks Bunking (`rax`):**
   - Each Barracks provides **75 ground housing space**.
   - Ground infantry (`sol`, `sci`, `tr`, `dr`, `ft`, `lt`, `ld`, `lf`) occupy **1 space**.
   - Tanks and Hover Tanks (`t`, `ht`) occupy **2 spaces**.
   - **Overcrowding Penalty:** If total ground military exceeds Barracks capacity, the surplus troops crowd into residential areas, reducing maximum civilian population on a 1-to-1 basis:
     $$\text{raxSurplus} = \max(0, \text{ground forces} - (\text{rax} \times 75))$$
     $$\text{maxPop} = \lfloor\text{res} \times \text{resCap} - \text{raxSurplus}\rfloor$$

2. **Air Support Bays (`asb`):**
   - Requires technology `r_f74` to construct.
   - Each Air Support Bay provides **60 hangar spaces**.
   - F-74 Drones (`f74`) occupy **1 hangar space**.
   - Tactical Fighters (`tf`) occupy **2 hangar spaces**.
   - **Hard Cap:** Air units cannot be trained if the total active plus queued aerospace units exceed total ASB capacity.

### Energy upkeep

Every active military unit consumes electrical power on every tick:
- **Infantry & Specialists** (`sol`, `sci`, `tr`, `dr`, `ft`, `lt`, `ld`, `lf`): **0.7 power / tick**.
- **Heavy Armor & Aircraft** (`t`, `ht`, `tf`, `f74`): **1.4 power / tick**.

If total power consumption exceeds Power Plant output, the kingdom's energy reserves will drain. When power drops to 0, economic production, research, and defensive systems suffer major operational disruptions.

---

### Automatic promotions

When a player completes a technical research milestone, existing standing troops in that branch are automatically upgraded to the newly discovered unit class without paying additional gold:
- Troopers (`tr`) $\to$ Dragoons (`dr`) upon completing `r_dr`.
- Dragoons (`dr`) $\to$ Fighters (`ft`) upon completing `r_ft`.
- Laser Troopers (`lt`) $\to$ Laser Dragoons (`ld`) upon completing `r_ld`.
- Laser Dragoons (`ld`) $\to$ Laser Fighters (`lf`) upon completing `r_lf`.
- Tanks (`t`) $\to$ Hover Tanks (`ht`) upon completing `r_ht`.

Any remainder soldier recruits from unequal ratio conversions are refunded back to the unassigned soldier pool (`military.sol`).

---

### Disbanding units

Rulers can decommission standing units to reduce upkeep or recover funds:
- **Gold Refund:** 50% of the unit's base gold cost ($\lfloor\text{base cost} / 2\rfloor$).
- **Personnel Recovery:** Units that required a soldier return **1 unassigned soldier recruit** to the pool.
- Disbanded units are removed immediately from the standing army.

---

### Combat potentials

The army's combat strength is evaluated across three primary metrics:
- **Maximum Offensive Potential:** Total offensive attack value of all active units (`tr`, `dr`, `ft`, `tf`, `t`, `ht`, `sol`), boosted by Military Strength research (`mil`).
- **Maximum Defensive Potential:** Total defensive defense value of all active units guarding the homeland (`lt`, `ld`, `lf`, `f74`, `t`, `ht`, `sol`), boosted by Military Strength research.
- **Minimum Defensive Potential (Home Defense):** Defense value of dedicated defensive units (`lt`, `ld`, `lf`, `f74`). Because offensive strike teams leave the kingdom during an attack, only dedicated defenders remain at home to protect against simultaneous counter-attacks.
