## Sector Leader Mass Messaging

Sector Leader Mass Messaging grants the democratically elected Sector Leader (SL) the privilege to broadcast announcements, defensive alerts, and strategic orders simultaneously to all kingdoms residing within their sector.

---

### Privilege & Authorization

- **Role Requirement:** Only the currently elected Sector Leader of the sector possesses access to the mass messaging control. Non-leaders see standard individual messaging options.
- **Scope Limit:** Broadcasts are restricted exclusively to kingdoms located within the SL's home sector. An SL cannot mass-message foreign sectors.

---

### Interface & Composition

1. **Broadcast Checkbox / Target Mode:**
   - In `SendMessages.aspx`, an elected Sector Leader has an exclusive toggle or recipient option: **"Send to entire Sector"** (or radio selector: *Individual Kingdom* vs *Sector Broadcast*).
   - Selecting this mode disables individual kingdom coordinates and automatically targets all active kingdoms in the SL's sector.
2. **Distinct Subject & Flagging:**
   - Outbound broadcasts are stamped with an authoritative prefix: `[SECTOR BROADCAST] <Subject>`.
   - The message sender clearly identifies the official title: `Sector Leader <Ruler Name> of <Kingdom Name> (<Sector:Kingdom>)`.
3. **Delivery:**
   - Upon dispatch, the system generates an inbox message in every kingdom's inbox within that sector (including bots/food kingdoms if configured, or all human-controlled kingdoms).

---

### Rate Limiting & Cooldowns

To prevent sector leaders from spamming or overwhelming sector member inboxes:

- **Cooldown Window:** Sector mass broadcasts enforce a cooldown period (e.g. 1 broadcast per hour, or once every 12 ticks), unless critical sector war alerts are declared.
- **Retention:** Broadcast messages follow the standard 3-day automatic inbox retention and deletion rule.
