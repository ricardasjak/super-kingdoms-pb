## Advanced technology research (fixed points)

Advanced technology research (technical research) represents permanent scientific breakthroughs with fixed point thresholds. Unlike land-based research, the research points required for technical topics do not scale with kingdom size and never dilute when land is acquired. Once completed, technologies remain at 100% permanently.

### Technology tree

```
[r_fusion] (30,000 pts)
   └── [r_core] (30,000 pts) ──┬── [r_armor] (92,000 pts)
                               ├── [r_f74] (200,000 pts) ──> [r_tf] (300,000 pts)
                               └── Unlocks land-based Warp Drive (`warp`)

[r_long] (30,000 pts) ──┬── [r_dr] (30,000 pts) ──> [r_ft] (80,000 pts)
                        └── [r_ld] (40,000 pts) ──> [r_lf] (80,000 pts)

[r_ht] (200,000 pts) [Standalone]
```

### Core mechanics

- **Fixed Point Requirement:** Every technology requires a constant number of scientist points ($30{,}000$ to $300{,}000$), unaffected by land expansion or kingdom networth.
- **Prerequisite Gating:** A technology cannot receive points until its prerequisite is at 100% completion. Players may only prioritize unlocked nodes.
- **Progress Formula:**
  $$\text{Progress \%} = \min\left(\left\lfloor \frac{\text{invested points}}{\text{Required Points}} \times 100 \right\rfloor, 100\right)$$
- **Auto-Assignment & Auto-Pruning:**
  - Scientist points are assigned in user-configured priority order (`researchAutoAssign`).
  - Unlike land-based research, when a technical node reaches 100%, it is **automatically removed** from the auto-assign priority queue on that tick.
- **Permanent Completion:** Once a technology reaches 100%, its bonuses and unlocks are permanent and never decay or dilute.

### Economic & civil bonuses

- **Power Plant Output:**
  - `r_fusion` grants a flat $+50\%$ boost (`fusionBonus = 0.50`).
  - `r_core` grants an additional flat $+20\%$ boost (`coreBonus = 0.20`).
  - These bonuses combine additively with the dynamic land-based Power research bonus (`powerBonus`):
    $$\text{Production} = \text{plants} \times 100 \times (1 + \text{powerBonus} + \text{fusionBonus} + \text{coreBonus})$$
- **Housing Expansion:**
  - `r_long` adds $+5$ base population capacity to every Residence (`resCapacity = 50 + 5 = 55`), which subsequently scales with standard Population research:
    $$\text{Effective Residence Capacity} = (50 + 5) \times (1 + \text{popBonus})$$

### Automatic unit promotions

When specific military technologies finish, the kingdom's existing standing army automatically upgrades lower-tier units to the newly unlocked tier. The unit pool points are converted and any remainder soldiers are refunded to the unassigned soldier pool (`military.sol`):

| Tech | Upgraded Unit | Target Unit | Pool Formula | Unit Received | Soldier Refund |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `r_dr` | Troopers (`tr`, ⚔️ 4) | Dragoons (`dr`, ⚔️ 5) | $\text{pool} = \text{tr} \times 4$ | $\lfloor\text{pool} / 5\rfloor$ | $\text{pool} \pmod 5$ |
| `r_ft` | Dragoons (`dr`, ⚔️ 5) | Fighters (`ft`, ⚔️ 6) | $\text{pool} = \text{dr} \times 5$ | $\lfloor\text{pool} / 6\rfloor$ | $\text{pool} \pmod 6$ |
| `r_ld` | Laser Troopers (`lt`, 🛡️ 4) | Laser Dragoons (`ld`, 🛡️ 5) | $\text{pool} = \text{lt} \times 4$ | $\lfloor\text{pool} / 5\rfloor$ | $\text{pool} \pmod 5$ |
| `r_lf` | Laser Dragoons (`ld`, 🛡️ 5) | Laser Fighters (`lf`, 🛡️ 6) | $\text{pool} = \text{ld} \times 5$ | $\lfloor\text{pool} / 6\rfloor$ | $\text{pool} \pmod 6$ |
| `r_ht` | Tanks (`t`, ⚔️ 9 / 🛡️ 9) | Hover Tanks (`ht`, ⚔️ 12 / 🛡️ 12) | $\text{pool} = \text{t} \times 9$ | $\lfloor\text{pool} / 12\rfloor$ | $\text{pool} \pmod 12$ |

*Note: After promotion completes, the predecessor unit count is set to 0.*

### Structural and unit unlocks

- **Air Force Infrastructure (`r_f74` & `r_tf`):**
  - Completing `r_f74` unlocks construction of Air Support Bays (`asb`), each housing up to 60 air units.
  - Completing `r_f74` unlocks recruitment of F-74 Drones (`f74`), requiring 1 ASB housing space, 1.4 power, and 0 soldiers.
  - Completing `r_tf` unlocks recruitment of Tactical Fighters (`tf`), requiring 2 ASB housing space, 1.4 power, and 0 soldiers.
- **Heavy Armor (`r_ht`):**
  - Unlocks recruitment of Hover Tanks (`ht`), requiring 2 Barracks housing space, 1 soldier, and 1.4 power.
- **Cross-Research Unlocks:**
  - Completing `r_core` unlocks the standard land-based research topic **Warp Drive** (`warp`), enabling faster hyperspace movement and offensive fleet speed.
