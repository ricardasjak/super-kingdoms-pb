# Game Design: Planet Types & Terrains

> **System**: Planetary Archetypes, Environmental Modifiers & Starter Packages  
> **Source of Truth**: [docs/game-manual/planettypes.md](../game-manual/planettypes.md)

---

## 1. Overview & Role

At kingdom creation, rulers select a homeworld archetype. Each of the 8 planet types provides a distinct set of passive bonuses, structural penalties, and upfront starter assets (initial troops, unlocked technologies, or pre-allocated research points), shaping the kingdom's developmental pathway and strategic playstyle.

---

## 2. Core Mechanics & Formulas

- **Permanent Archetype**:
  - The chosen planet type remains locked for the duration of the round.
- **Modifier Stacking**:
  - Percentage advantages and disadvantages apply multiplicatively or additively with research bonuses and sector states.
- **Starter Packages**:
  - New kingdoms spawn with specific research projects pre-completed or dedicated military units stationed in their garrisons on day one.

## 4. System Synergies & Strategic Alignments

- **Warmonger / Rush Builds**: *Desert Wasteland* (-90% infantry cost, +30% offense) and *Mystical Lands* (-30% attack march time) excel at rapid conquest.
- **Economic / Industrial Booms**: *Forest and Wilderness* (huge population cap and extra Star Mine yield) and *Mountainous* (+25% income, +75% war honor) provide top-tier credit generation.
- **Technological Hegemony**: *Terra Form* allows scientific kingdoms to break standard research caps and hit maximum bonuses significantly faster.
- **Fortress Kingdoms**: *Oceanic* and *Volcanic Inferno* combine high natural defenses, cheap infrastructure, or massive energy storage reserves.
