## Scores Archive & Career Stats

The Scores Archive (`ScoresArchive.aspx`) and Career Statistics (`CareerStats.aspx`) provide persistent, long-term historical records of past game rounds and cumulative player achievements.

While live leaderboards track the active round, the archive immortalizes round champions, alliance dynasties, and individual player performance across the entire lifespan of the universe.

---

### Archive Navigation & Filtering

The historical archive view (`ScoresArchive.aspx`) enables players to explore past rounds through specialized multi-dimensional filters:

1. **Board Dropdown (`score` parameter):**
   - **Domination (`score=9`):** End-of-round individual domination champions.
   - **Alliance Domination (`score=10`):** Winning alliances that secured galactic victory.
   - **Networth (`score=1`):** Final economic and military powerhouses.
   - **Land (`score=2`):** Final territorial size rankings.
   - **Honor (`score=3`):** Battle honor champions.
   - **Hall of Fame (`score=4`):** Overall composite excellence rankings.
   - **Leanest (`score=5`):** Efficiency and concentration champions.
   - **Robbers (`score=6`):** Plunder and economic raid masters.
   - **Alliances (`score=7`):** Final alliance cumulative standings.

2. **Round Selector:**
   - Dropdown listing all concluded game rounds (e.g. `R117`, `R116`, `R115`, ..., down to `R1`).
   - Quick navigation step buttons: `<<` (prior round) and `>>` (subsequent round).

3. **Top Filter:**
   - Options to filter by tier: `Top 100`, `Top 250`, or `All Ranked`.

4. **Breakdown View Toggle:**
   - A `Show breakdown` toggle switch revealing sub-component point distributions (e.g. baseline score, bonus multipliers, relic faith points, and territory contributions).

---

### Archive Leaderboard Table

The archive table displays immutable snapshots of final standings at round conclusion:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **RANK** | Final placement in the selected category board (e.g. 1st, 2nd, 3rd, ...). |
| **PLAYER** | Account username of the player / commander. |
| **KINGDOM** | Final kingdom name, ruler title, and sector coordinates at round conclusion. |
| **TOTAL** | Final audited score, networth, acreage, or points tally. |

- **Sorting:** Interactive sorting by rank or total score.
- **Pagination:** Paged view (default 25 rows per page) with numbered jump links.

---

### Career Statistics (`CareerStats.aspx`)

Career Stats summarize an account's legacy across all participation history:

1. **Round Participation:**
   - Number of active rounds played, victory crowns earned, and podium finishes (Top 3 / Top 10).
2. **All-Time Accolades & Records:**
   - Peak networth achieved in any single round.
   - Peak land acreage colonized.
   - Highest battle honor achieved.
   - Total enemy units destroyed and sectors liberated.
3. **Alliance Legacy:**
   - Alliances founded or joined that captured round victory or domination banners.
