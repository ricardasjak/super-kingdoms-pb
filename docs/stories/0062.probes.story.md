## Probes

Probes are produced by Probe Factories and used for covert missions against other kingdoms (intel gathering and aggressive operations), and for mining gold.

> Draft: mission descriptions are taken from the in-game Probe help. Success chances, attempt limits and report contents are still to be confirmed.

### Production

- Each Probe Factory produces **0.75 probes per tick**.
- **Production slowdown** applies when a kingdom holds more than 20 probes per acre of land (anti probe-bomb):
  - `decrease_factor = min((probes_per_land - 20)^2 / 500, 0.75)`
  - Production is reduced by this factor (max 75% reduction).

### Missions

The player picks a target kingdom, a mission type, and the number of probes to send. Optional **Shields ON** reduces probe losses by 50% and costs power (shown on the page, e.g. "Shields would cost 1,750 power (you have 40,000)").

| Mission | Effect (from in-game help) |
|---|---|
| Spy on Kingdom | Decrypt detailed information of a kingdom |
| Spy on Military | Decrypt the military status of a kingdom |
| Spy on Buildings | Decrypt the current buildings of a kingdom |
| Spy on Research | Decrypt the current research of a kingdom |
| Spy on Shields | Decrypt the current shield % of a kingdom |
| Spy on News | Decrypt the current news of an alliance |
| Mine Gold | See *Mining gold* below |
| Rob Banks | Returns 10 money per probe sent, up to 10% of the defender's money |
| Arson | Destroy an enemy's residences |
| Sabotage Powerplants | Destroy an enemy's power plants |
| Kidnappings | Return 1/4th of the 5% of stolen population |
| Suicide Probes | Sacrifice your probes to destroy the enemy's current power, 7 power per probe |

- The six spy missions are non-aggressive; Rob, Arson, Sabotage, Kidnappings and Suicide are aggressive.
- **Probe defense:** 4x land is the probe defense that land provides; 20x land is the maximum probe defense of a kingdom. The page offers quick options that set the probe count by a land multiple.
- Probe missions and probes in general are affected by planet type, race, kingdom state, stratagems, modules and more (details to be captured in those stories).
- A **Show history** toggle lists past probe missions (columns not captured).
- Mission outcome details (success chance, what each report shows, attempt limits) are **to be confirmed**.

### Probe losses

Losses are a percentage of probes sent, applied after each mission.

- Base loss: `1.1% * (1.5 - 0.25 * random + 0.25 * random)` (average ≈ 1.65%).
- Aggressive missions (rob, sabotage): losses **+50%**.
- Failed mission: losses **x2**.
- Shielded probes: losses **x0.5**.
- Probe armour: losses **x0.5** (multiplies with shields).
- Other loss modifiers (e.g. Shadow research, -10% per level) are **additive** after the multipliers.

Example: robbing, 10,000 probes, shielded, probe armour, failed, Shadow 1 → 1.65 → x1.5 = 2.475 → x0.5 = 1.2375 → x0.5 = 0.61875 → x2 = 1.2375 → -0.1 = 1.11375% → **111 probes lost**.

### Mining gold (related)

Probes can also be used to mine gold (see the separate Mine Gold story, #2 in the coverage plan).

- Gold per mission by probe count: 0–15,000: `6.5 × probes`; 15,001–30,000: `-9,809,020 × probes^-0.378512 + 355,088`; 30,001–859,400: `14.1692 × probes^0.847744 + 68,473.3`; 859,401+: `1.5 × probes + 300,000`.
- Cap per tick: `100,000 + Residences × 50 + Star Mines × 100`.
- Shielded probes have 60% effectiveness for gold-mining losses; unshielded losses are higher.
