## Sector Politics & Sector Leader Elections

Sector Politics (`Politics.aspx`) governs local galactic democracy. Every sector operates as a self-governing political unit where member kingdoms democratically elect their **Sector Leader (SL)** (also referred to as President / Sector Governor).

The elected Sector Leader represents the sector, coordinates regional defense, broadcasts sector-wide directives, and customizes the sector's public presence.

---

### Sector Leader Election Mechanics

Each sector runs recurring or continuous election cycles to determine its leadership:

1. **Eligibility & Candidacy:**
   - Any active, non-newbie kingdom located within the sector is eligible to run for Sector Leader or receive votes.
   - Protected newbie kingdoms (`Newbie` mode) cannot hold the Sector Leader office until their protection expires or is manually dropped.
2. **Voting Procedure (`Politics.aspx`):**
   - Each kingdom residing in the sector holds exactly **1 vote**.
   - A kingdom casts its vote by selecting a candidate kingdom from a dropdown menu of sector residents and clicking `Vote`.
   - A kingdom may vote for itself or any fellow sector resident.
   - Votes can be changed by the voter at any time during an active term.
3. **Winning Threshold & Election Tally:**
   - **Plurality / Majority Rule:** The candidate receiving the highest number of votes (or exceeding a minimum required threshold, e.g. >50% or highest vote count) assumes the title of Sector Leader.
   - **Tie-Breaker:** In the event of a tie, the candidate with higher networth (or longer residency in the sector) prevails.
   - **Vacant / Uncontested Sectors:** If no votes are cast, the sector remains without an active leader, or leadership defaults to the highest-networth non-bot kingdom.

---

### Sector Leader Powers & Privileges

Once elected, the Sector Leader gains exclusive administrative and tactical capabilities:

1. **Sector Message of the Day (MotD) / Banner:**
   - The SL can author and update the sector's public announcement message displayed in [Sector View](0064.sector-view.story.md) (`MySector.aspx`).
   - Allows sharing Discord server links, alliance recruitment slogans, internal sector treaties, or target coordination notes.
2. **Sector Leader Mass Messaging:**
   - Exclusive access to dispatch [Sector Leader Mass Broadcasts](0071.sector-leader-mass-messaging.story.md) directly to all member inboxes.
3. **Prestige & Identification:**
   - The kingdom's ruler receives the prestigious `[Sector Leader]` badge in the Sector View kingdoms table and universal rankings.
   - Distinctive icon or highlight distinguishing the leader's kingdom.

---

### Term Limits & Deposing an Ineffective Leader

- **Vote Fluidity:** If a leader becomes inactive, turns hostile, or falls in battle, sector members can redirect their votes to another resident.
- **Immediate Transition:** As soon as an alternate candidate overtakes the incumbent's vote tally upon the next tick evaluation (or instantaneously), leadership privileges transfer immediately to the newly elected leader.
