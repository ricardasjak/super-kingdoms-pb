## Sector Leader (SL)

The Sector Leader (SL) is the head of state and military commander for an entire sector. Elected democratically by member kingdoms, the Sector Leader directs foreign policy, decides alliance membership, coordinates regional defense, and receives a combat bonus in battle.

---

### Election & Tie-Breaking

1. **Member Voting:**
   - Every active kingdom residing within the sector casts one vote for their chosen Sector Leader candidate.
   - Any resident non-newbie kingdom is eligible to receive votes and hold the office.
   - Members can change their vote at any time via `Politics.aspx`.
2. **Plurality & Networth Tie-Breaker:**
   - The candidate with the highest number of votes becomes the Sector Leader.
   - **Tie-Breaking Rule:** If two or more candidate kingdoms receive an equal number of votes, the kingdom with the **larger Networth (NW)** becomes the Sector Leader.
3. **Dynamic Succession:**
   - If an incumbent's vote count is surpassed by a challenger (or overtaken in NW during a tie), the Sector Leader title, combat bonus, and administrative powers transfer immediately to the new leader.

---

### Military Attacking Bonus

Holding the office of Sector Leader inspires the kingdom's military forces and provides tactical command advantages:

- **+10% Attacking Bonus:** The Sector Leader's military receives a flat **+10% attacking bonus** when launching offensive attacks against enemy kingdoms.
- **Additive Stacking:** This bonus is strictly **additive** with other modifiers (e.g. racial bonuses, research bonuses, stratagems, and battle honor), rather than multiplicative.
  - *Example:* If a kingdom has a +5% racial attack bonus and earns the SL office (+10%), their total attack modifier is `1.00 + 0.05 + 0.10 = 1.15` (+15%).
- **Offensive Scope:** Applies to offensive military strikes launched while the kingdom currently holds the SL office.

---

### Alliance Membership Governance

The Sector Leader holds the sole executive authority to manage alliance diplomacy for their sector:

1. **Joining an Alliance:**
   - The Sector Leader can apply to or accept an invitation to join an alliance on behalf of the sector.
   - When the SL joins an alliance, the sector becomes an official constituent of that alliance.
2. **Leaving an Alliance:**
   - The Sector Leader can withdraw the sector from an alliance at any time, returning the sector to independent non-aligned status.
3. **Leadership Prerequisite:**
   - Regular sector members cannot join or leave alliances independently; alliance alignment is determined collectively at the sector level by the Sector Leader.

---

### Additional Powers & Privileges

- **Sector Broadcasts:** Authority to send high-priority [Sector Leader Mass Messages](0071.sector-leader-mass-messaging.story.md) to all sector members.
- **Sector Message of the Day (MotD):** Edit and maintain the public announcement banner in [Sector View](0064.sector-view.story.md) (`MySector.aspx`).
- **Identification:** Displays the prominent `[Sector Leader]` badge on kingdom profiles, sector listings, and leaderboards.
