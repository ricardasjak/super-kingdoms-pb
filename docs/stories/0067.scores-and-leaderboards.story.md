## Scores & Live Leaderboards

The Scores & Leaderboards hub (`Scores.aspx`, `ScoresDomination.aspx`, and category rankings) serves as the universal ranking center of the game universe. It tracks player and alliance progression, military accolades, economic strength, and galactic domination in real time across the active round.

---

### Overview & Navigation Structure

The Scores hub provides a multi-tab interface organized into three primary operational views:

1. **Leaderboards Tab:** Live rankings across all competitive gameplay dimensions during the active round.
2. **Archives Tab:** Historical round results and player career tracking (detailed in [0068.scores-archive.story.md](0068.scores-archive.story.md)).
3. **Data Exports Tab:** Raw universe data dumps for external alliance spreadsheets, Discord bots, and community tooling.

---

### Domination Overview & Widget

At the top of the Scores screen, the **Domination Leaderboard** summary highlights the premier competitive race for galactic control:

- **Domination Points Mechanics:** Points accumulated by sectors holding relics, controlling strategic objectives, and maintaining high sector networth.
- **Top Alliances Card:** The 3 highest-scoring alliances with their current Domination points.
- **Top Kingdoms Card:** The 3 highest-scoring kingdoms in the galaxy.
- **User Rank Banner:** A personalized status badge indicating the player's current universal rank, total domination points, and margin to the next rank tier.
- **Full Board Action:** A button linking to the comprehensive galactic domination standings (`ScoresDomination.aspx`).

### Universe Leaderboard Categories

The universal leaderboards are organized into distinct category boards, accessible as quick-launch cards or filtered views:

| Category | Primary Metric | Target Page / Board | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Domination** | Domination Points | `ScoresDomination.aspx` | Overall round score factoring relics, alliances, and territorial dominance. |
| **Strongest Kingdoms** | Total Networth | `UniverseNetworth.aspx` | Economic and military capital combined. Primary measure of raw kingdom size. |
| **Largest Kingdoms** | Land Acreage | `UniverseLand.aspx` | Total territorial acreage colonized or captured. |
| **Most Honorable** | Battle Honor Points | `ScoresWarHonor.aspx` | Honor earned through PvP combat victories against worthy opponents. |
| **Top Land Gain** | Acres Captured | `LargestLandGrab.aspx` | Single-strike or single-tick military conquest records. |
| **Most Kills** | Enemy Casualties | `MostKills.aspx` | Total enemy units destroyed in offensive campaigns and defensive holds. |
| **Leanest Kingdoms** | Networth / Land Ratio | `UniverseNwLandRatio.aspx` | Economic density and unit concentration per acre of land. |
| **Most Aggressive Probing** | Espionage Probes | `AggressiveProbings.aspx` | Intelligence probes successfully launched against target kingdoms. |
| **Most Worthy** | Hall of Fame Points | `ScoresHoF.aspx` | Dynamic composite scoring measuring efficiency, combat prowess, and growth. |
| **Most Faithful** | Faith / Relic Points | `ScoresFaith.aspx` | Religious / relic adherence points generated by holy relics. |
| **Top Alliances** | Total Alliance Networth / Points | `StrongestAlliances.aspx` | Cumulative standing and total military strength of registered alliances. |

### Leaderboard Table Standard Features

Each universal ranking board provides standardized table capabilities:

- **Universal Coordinates:** Displays each kingdom's exact galactic coordinates (`Galaxy:Sector`, e.g. `1:9`) with a direct link to jump to that sector in the [Sector View](0064.sector-view.story.md).
- **Player & Kingdom Identity:** Kingdom name (linking to profile) and ruler name. Current viewer's kingdom is highlighted with an active accent row.
- **Alliance Affiliation:** Alliance tag in brackets `[TAG]` linking to Alliance Info.
- **Mode & Status Badges:** Clear indicators for protected states (`Newbie (N)`, `PvE`, `FOOD`, or `War`).
- **Real-Time Delta Indicators:** Networth and land changes over recent ticks (e.g. green `↑1,015` or red `↓698`).
- **Pagination & Rows Per Page:** Supports 25, 50, and 100 rows per page with numbered navigation buttons and quick jump.

### Data Exports (Universe Dump)

To support third-party alliance tools, war room planners, and Discord bots:

- **Export Endpoint (`Data_v3.aspx`):** Provides structured universe dumps containing sector numbers, kingdom coordinates, names, networth, land, and alliance tags.
- **Tick Verification:** Includes the current game tick timestamp to prevent stale cache deserialization in client tooling.
- **Throttling & Security:** Rate-limited and cached on server tick cycles to eliminate database strain.
