## Military Decommissioning & Force Disbandment

The Military Disbandment protocol enables rulers to demobilize standing armed forces, liquidating surplus units to recover treasury gold, relieve power grid upkeep, and reallocate personnel back to the civilian workforce or base recruit reserves.

---

### Core Mechanics & Recovery Rules

When military units are disbanded, demobilization is **immediate** (bypassing training pipelines) and yields two distinct forms of recovery:

1. **Treasury Gold Refund**:
   - Every disbanded unit returns **25% of its base recruitment gold cost**:
     $$\text{Gold Refund} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Base Unit Cost}}{4} \right\rfloor$$
   - Training Camp (`tc`) discounts do not diminish the disband refund; the refund is consistently calculated from the unit's base price.

2. **Personnel Reallocation**:
   - **Specialized Combatants & Armored Units** (`tr`, `dr`, `ft`, `lt`, `ld`, `lf`, `t`, `ht`): Disbanding returns **1 unassigned soldier recruit** (`military.sol`) per unit back to the standing reserve pool.
   - **Base Soldiers** (`sol`): Disbanding unassigned recruits returns them directly to civilian life, increasing total kingdom **civilian population** (`population`).
   - **Aerospace & Autonomous Craft** (`tf`, `f74`): As uncrewed or manufactured hardware, they require 0 soldier recruits to produce and therefore return **no personnel** upon scrapping.
   - **Scientists** (`sci`): Commissioned non-combatants return their gold refund without personnel recovery.

3. **Immediate Upkeep & Housing Relief**:
   - Disbanded units instantly cease consuming power (0.7 power/tick for infantry and scientists; 1.4 power/tick for armor and aerospace craft).
   - Housing capacity in Barracks or Aerospace Bases is immediately freed.
