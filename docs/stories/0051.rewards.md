## Daily Kingdom Rewards

To encourage daily player engagement and provide strategic momentum, rulers receive a daily royal supply grant. Once every 24-hour cycle, a ruler can choose **one of three** tailored supply packages to accelerate their kingdom's growth, fortify their economy, or strengthen tactical reconnaissance.

---

### Core Cadence & Reset Rules

- **Frequency**: A player may claim a reward **once per 24-hour cycle**.
- **Global Reset Time**: The reward cycle resets globally every day at **08:00 AM UTC** (08:00:00 UTC).
- **Eligibility**: Strictly available to kingdoms in **PvP Mode** (`kd-pvpmode`). Rewards are **locked and unavailable** while a kingdom is in **Newbie Mode** (`kd-newbiemode`) or **PvE Mode** (`kd-pvemode`).
- **Single Choice**: Upon opening the rewards depot, the player selects exactly **one** of the three available rewards.
- **No Stacking**: Unclaimed rewards do not bank, accumulate, or roll over. If a player does not claim during a given 24-hour cycle, the grant is replaced by the new cycle's grant at 08:00 AM UTC.
- **Countdown Display**: The UI clearly displays a live countdown clock indicating time remaining until the next 08:00 UTC reset.

---

### The Three Reward Options

| Reward | Base Amount | Mathematical Formula | Strategic Focus |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **1. Treasury Infusion** | $3\times$ Hourly Income | $\text{Gold Grant} = 3 \times \text{moneyIncome}$ | Rapid funding for building queues, military recruitment, or research |
| **2. Territorial Expansion** | $1\%$ of Land (min 20) | $\text{Land Grant} = \max(20, \lfloor \text{land} \times 0.01 \rfloor)$ | Territorial growth without gold cost or exploration queue delay |
| **3. Reconnaissance Probes** | 6 Ticks Probes + 6 Attempts | $\text{Probes} + (6 \times \text{pf})$, $\text{Attempts} = \min(10, \text{curr} + 6)$ | Espionage intel, target reconnaissance, and tactical raid preparation |

---

### Mechanics & Formulas

#### 1. Treasury Infusion (Money Reward)
- **Effect**: Deposits an instant lump sum of gold directly into the kingdom's treasury.
- **Value**: Exactly **3 times the kingdom's current tick/hourly money income**.
- **Calculation**:
  $$\text{Income} = \left(\text{Star Mines} \times 140 + \text{Population} \times 2\right) \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Research Money \%}}{100}\right)$$
  $$\text{Reward Gold} = 3 \times \text{Income}$$
- **Dynamic Preview**: The UI displays the exact calculated gold amount based on current economic output before the player confirms their pick.

#### 2. Territorial Expansion (Land Reward)
- **Effect**: Immediately awards undeveloped acreage directly to total kingdom territory (`land`).
- **Value**: **1% of current kingdom land**, with a guaranteed floor of **at least 20 acres**.
- **Calculation**:
  $$\text{Reward Land} = \max\left(20, \left\lfloor \text{current land} \times 0.01 \right\rfloor\right)$$
- **Benefits**:
  - Newly acquired land is added immediately to available unbuilt land (bypassing the 24-hour exploration queue).
  - High relative value for new or developing kingdoms (e.g. for a 250-acre kingdom, +20 land represents an immediate +8.0% territorial boost).

#### 3. Reconnaissance Probes (Probes & Attempts Reward)
- **Effect**: Delivers a two-part espionage reinforcement:
  1. **Probe Inventory**: Instantly manufactures **6 ticks worth of probe production** directly into the kingdom's probe stockpile (`probes`).
  2. **Launch Attempts**: Grants **6 probe launch attempts** added to tactical operational reserves.
- **Cap**: Probe attempts are strictly **capped at a maximum of 10**. (The physical probe inventory itself has no 10-unit cap).
- **Calculation**:
  $$\text{Probes Granted} = 6 \times \text{Probe Production} = 6 \times \text{Probe Factories (pf)}$$
  $$\text{New Probe Attempts} = \min\left(10, \text{current probe attempts} + 6\right)$$
- **Cap & Minimum Rules**:
  - If a player holds 5 attempts, claiming this grants +5 attempts to hit the ceiling of 10.
  - If a player already has 10 attempts, only the physical probes are credited, and the attempts meter indicates "Attempts Capped (10/10)".
  - For kingdoms without Probe Factories ($\text{pf} = 0$), a baseline floor can be guaranteed (e.g., minimum 6 probes, matching 1 baseline factory rate) alongside the 6 launch attempts.

### Player Journey & User Interface

1. **Daily Requisition Banner / Widget**:
   - Displayed prominently in the Kingdom dashboard / header navigation.
   - **Mode Restrictions**:
     - In **Newbie Mode** or **PvE Mode**, the widget displays a locked state: *"Daily supply rewards unlock upon graduating to PvP Mode."*
     - In **PvP Mode**, displays badge status: **"Reward Available!"** when eligible, or a countdown timer (`Resets in 14h 22m`) when already claimed for the current cycle.

2. **Selection Modal / Cards**:
   - Three interactive option cards displayed side-by-side:
     - **Card 1: Gold Supply** — Displays current income, multiplier badge ($3\times$), and the exact gold payout (e.g. `+142,800 Gold`).
     - **Card 2: Land Survey** — Displays current territory, percentage rate ($1\%$, min 20), and the exact land payout (e.g. `+20 Acres` or `+64 Acres`).
     - **Card 3: Reconnaissance Package** — Displays both components:
       - Probe units: `+6 Ticks Production` (e.g. `+48 Probes` based on Probe Factories).
       - Attempts meter: `+6 Attempts` (e.g. `3 / 10 → 9 / 10`).

3. **Claim Confirmation**:
   - The player selects one card and clicks **"Claim Daily Reward"**.
   - Upon confirmation:
     - Resources, land, or probe reserves update immediately.
     - A notification toast confirms receipt: e.g. *"Claimed Daily Reward: +36 Probes & +6 Probe Attempts"*.
     - Cards enter disabled state showing *"Claimed for today. Next reward available at 08:00 UTC."*

---

### State & Lifecycle Rules

- **Kingdom Modes Eligibility**:
  - **PvP Mode Only**: Daily rewards are strictly available to active kingdoms in **PvP Mode** (`kd-pvpmode`).
  - **Unavailable in Newbie Mode & PvE Mode**: Kingdoms currently in **Newbie Mode** (`kd-newbiemode`) or **PvE Mode** (`kd-pvemode`) **cannot claim daily rewards**.
  - **Dead & Inactive Kingdoms**: Kingdoms in `kd-dead` or `kd-inactive` status cannot claim daily rewards.
- **Mode Graduation**:
  - When a kingdom finishes PvE mode (tick 72) and graduates into PvP mode, daily rewards become unlocked and claimable for the ongoing 24-hour cycle.
- **Reset Boundary**:
  - The cycle is determined by the server's UTC time.
  - At `08:00:00 UTC`, the server flips the daily claim status for the new 24-hour window. If a player is online at 08:00 UTC, the UI refreshes to make the new reward available.

### Example Scenarios

#### Scenario A: Newly Graduated Kingdom (Entry PvP Stage)
- **Stats**: 450 acres land, 4,500 population, 6 star mines, 2 probe factories, 0 probe attempts.
- **Hourly Income**: $(6 \times 140 + 4,500 \times 2) = 9,840\text{ gold}$.
- **Probe Output**: 2 probes/tick.
- **Reward Choices**:
  1. **Money**: $3 \times 9,840 = \mathbf{29,520\text{ gold}}$
  2. **Land**: $\max(20, \lfloor 450 \times 0.01 \rfloor) = \max(20, 4) = \mathbf{20\text{ acres}}$ *(+4.4% land growth)*
  3. **Probes**: $6 \times 2 = \mathbf{12\text{ probes}}$ and $\min(10, 0 + 6) = \mathbf{6\text{ probe attempts}}$
- **Player Decision**: Likely chooses **Land** (+20 acres) for rapid expansion into competitive PvP territory.

#### Scenario B: Established Empire (Late Stage PvP)
- **Stats**: 8,500 acres land, 85,000 population, 80 star mines (+20% money research), 25 probe factories, 3 probe attempts.
- **Hourly Income**: $(80 \times 140 + 85,000 \times 2) \times 1.20 = 217,440\text{ gold}$.
- **Probe Output**: 25 probes/tick.
- **Reward Choices**:
  1. **Money**: $3 \times 217,440 = \mathbf{652,320\text{ gold}}$
  2. **Land**: $\max(20, \lfloor 8,500 \times 0.01 \rfloor) = \mathbf{85\text{ acres}}$
  3. **Probes**: $6 \times 25 = \mathbf{150\text{ probes}}$ and $\min(10, 3 + 6) = \mathbf{9\text{ probe attempts}}$
- **Player Decision**: Likely chooses **Probes** or **Money** depending on whether preparing reconnaissance for major alliance operations.
