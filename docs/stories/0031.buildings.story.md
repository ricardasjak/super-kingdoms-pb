## Kingdom buildings

Buildings develop explored land and provide economy, energy, military capacity, and intelligence.

### Building types

| Building | Key | Effect / Capacity | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residences | `res` | +50 pop capacity (boosted by Pop tech), provides civilian tax income (2 gold per pop/tick) | None |
| Power Plants | `plants` | +100 power/tick, +1,000 power storage capacity (boosted by Power techs) | None |
| Barracks | `rax` | +75 ground military housing space (soldiers, troopers, droids, tanks, scientists) | None |
| Star Mines | `sm` | +120 gold income/tick (boosted by Economy tech) | None |
| Probe Factories | `pf` | +1 spy probe produced per tick | None |
| Training Camps | `tc` | Up to 30% discount on military training cost: `min(30%, floor((tc / land) * 300))` | None |
| Air Support Bays | `asb` | +60 air unit housing space (Tactical Fighters & F-74 Interceptors) | Tech `r_f74` |

### Construction & land rules

- Each building occupies 1 acre of land.
- Free land: `land - (sum(built) + sum(queued) + rubble)`. Buildings can only be built on free land.
- Construction time: **16 ticks**, queued and completed evenly across the 16-tick pipeline.
- Construction cost per building: `round(sqrt(land) * 63)` gold.
- Razing buildings: refunds 25% of current construction cost (`floor(cost / 4)`) and converts razed structures into **rubble**.
- Rubble occupies land until cleared or redeveloped.
- Auto-builder: kingdoms can configure zoning percentage targets (0–100%) to automatically queue construction on each tick when enabled and affordable.

### Razing buildings

When a ruler wishes to tear down operational buildings to immediately recover a portion of their wealth or rezone land, they can raze existing structures.

#### Core rules & mechanics

- **Immediate Cash Refund (25%)**: Razing a building liquidates materials and refunds **25% of the current construction cost**:
  $$\text{Refund per Building} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Current Building Cost}}{4} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{round}(\sqrt{\text{land}} \times 63)}{4} \right\rfloor$$
- **Rubble Generation**: Demolishing structures leaves debris behind. Each razed building is instantly converted into 1 acre of **rubble**.
  - Rubble continues to occupy kingdom land until cleared or redeveloped with new construction.
- **Immediate Effect**:
  - The razed buildings are removed instantly from the kingdom's active building counts.
  - All bonuses, capacities (housing, energy storage, military space), and productions provided by the razed structures cease immediately.
  - The 25% gold refund is credited to the kingdom treasury immediately upon demolition.
- **Universal Application**: Unlike conversion, all operational building types (including Power Plants) can be razed.

### Converting buildings

When a ruler needs to retool their kingdom's strategy (e.g., shifting from an economic focus of Star Mines to wartime Barracks), they can convert existing built structures into a different building type instead of razing them.

#### Core rules & restrictions

- **Material Reuse & Cost Savings**: Converting recycles existing foundations and materials. The gold cost is **50% of the standard construction cost**:
  $$\text{Conversion Cost} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Standard Building Cost}}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{round}(\sqrt{\text{land}} \times 63)}{2} \right\rfloor$$
- **Power Plant Exception**: Power Plants use entirely different materials and radiation-shielded architecture. Therefore:
  - Power Plants **cannot** be converted into any other building type.
  - No other building type **can** be converted into a Power Plant.
- **Conversion Duration**: Conversion takes **20 ticks** (20 hours) to complete (subject to active construction speed / building time reductions).
- **Prerequisites**: Target building requirements must still be satisfied (e.g., converting into Air Support Bays still requires the `r_f74` technology).
- **Capacity & Lifecycle**:
  - Source buildings are removed from active service immediately upon starting conversion (their capacities and benefits cease).
  - Target buildings become operational immediately upon completion of the 20-tick pipeline.
  - Does not generate rubble; land stays occupied by the conversion pipeline throughout the transition.
