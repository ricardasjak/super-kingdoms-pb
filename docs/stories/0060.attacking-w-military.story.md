## Attacking with Military

Every kingdom has 4 war leaders (WLs) available for attacks. At least one leader must be used to attack another kingdom.
Higher number of WLs helps to get army back faster.

WL(nth) = Math.floor((time / wlsUsed) * nth)
for example:
return time | wls used | wls return times
8 | 4 | 2,4,6,8
8 | 3 | 2,5,8
8 | 2 | 4,8
8 | 1 | 8

10 | 4 | 2,5,7,10
10 | 3 | 3,6,10
10 | 2 | 5,10
10 | 1 | 10

12 | 4 | 3,6,9,12
12 | 3 | 4,8,12
12 | 2 | 6,12
12 | 1 | 12
