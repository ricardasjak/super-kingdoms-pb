## Land-based research

Land-based (standard) research topics provide percentage bonuses that scale dynamically with the kingdom's land size. Unlike advanced technologies with fixed research points, the points required for standard research increase as the kingdom expands.

### Research topics

| Topic | Key | Weight | Max Bonus | Effect | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Population | `pop` | 0.00475 | +20% | Increases residence housing capacity and maximum population | None |
| Power | `power` | 0.00311 | +50% | Increases energy output from Power Plants | None |
| Military Strength | `mil` | 0.00291 | +30% | Multiplies offensive and defensive army combat effectiveness | None |
| Money / Economy | `money` | 0.00535 | +25% | Multiplies gold income from population taxes and Star Mines | None |
| Frequency Decryption | `fdc` | 0.00040 | +25% | Improves communication security and espionage probe defense | None |
| Warp Drive | `warp` | 0.00173 | +20% | Enhances offensive attack efficiency and fleet travel | Tech `r_core` |

### Core mechanics

- **Point Generation:** Each scientist (`sci`) produces 1 research point per tick.
- **Cost Formula:** Required points scale quadratically with land:
  $$\text{Required Points} = \text{round}(\text{land}^2 \times \text{weight})$$
- **Bonus Calculation:**
  $$\text{Current Bonus \%} = \min\left(\left\lfloor \frac{\text{Max Bonus} \times \text{invested points}}{\text{Required Points}} \right\rfloor, \text{Max Bonus}\right)$$
- **Land Dilution:** When a kingdom acquires land through exploration or war, required points increase, diluting the effective bonus percentage until more points are invested.
- **Auto-Assignment:** Players can set a priority list (`researchAutoAssign`) to automatically allocate scientist points on each tick up to the required points for each topic.
