## Game News & Previous News

Game News and Previous News (`News.aspx` / Previous News Drawer) provide kingdoms with access to historical event archives and server-wide announcements, distinct from the active, tactical feed of the [Kingdom News Room](0065.kingdom-news.story.md).

While Kingdom News focuses on immediate, active-tick military engagements (incoming attacks, recent probes, and missile impacts), **Previous News** preserves older event logs from prior days/sessions and broadcasts game-wide announcements.

---

### Access and Interface Layout

In the modernized interface, Previous News is accessible persistently across all pages:

1. **Persistent Top Bar Trigger:**
   - Located on the top utility bar (represented by a newspaper/card icon alongside the AFK toggle and notification bell).
   - Clicking opens the **Previous News** drawer overlay from the right edge of the screen, allowing players to review history without navigating away from their current gameplay screen.

2. **Drawer Components:**
   - **Header:** Displays `Previous News` with a newspaper icon and a close (`X`) button.
   - **Copy Utility:** A `Copy News` button allows one-click export of selected or all archived entries directly to the system clipboard (formatted for Discord or forum sharing).
   - **News Table:**
     - Master `Select all` checkbox in table header.
     - `WHEN`: Timestamp and game tick reference of the archived entry.
     - `REPORT`: Narrative event text or announcement body.
     - **Empty State:** Shows *"No news yet."* when no historical logs or announcements are active.

---

### Event Types and Archive Categories

1. **Daily / Historical Rollover Archive:**
   - As ticks advance and daily rollovers occur, older tactical news items from the Kingdom News feed automatically roll over into the Previous News archive.
   - Prevents the primary combat feed from becoming cluttered while retaining a verifiable combat audit trail.

2. **Global & Server Announcements:**
   - **Round Lifecycle Events:** Round start announcements, scheduled end-of-round countdowns, and maintenance warnings.
   - **Celestial Events:** Global environmental modifiers affecting the galaxy (e.g. *Active: Gravity Wave for 16 ticks*, Solar Storms, Cosmic Radiation).
   - **Galaxy Milestones:** Public broadcasts when relics change hands or alliances achieve major galactic milestones.
   - **Patch Notes & Rule Updates:** System updates posted by game administration.

---

### Integration and Clipboard Export

- **Read-Only Display:** The news drawer is strictly read-only; players do not post replies or comments directly within this view.
- **Copy News Utility:** Formats selected rows into clean text blocks with timestamps, attacker/defender coordinates, and outcomes for easy pasting into alliance Discord war rooms or private messaging.
